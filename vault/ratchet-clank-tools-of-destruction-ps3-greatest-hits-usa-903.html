PlayStation 3Greatest HitsUSA (NTSC-U)2009

North American Greatest Hits reissue, released on June 16, 2009. On PlayStation 3, Sony did not assign new product codes to its Greatest Hits: it is the same disc and the same barcode as the original edition, in a red case with a banner. A bilingual Canadian variant exists, titled Grands Succès.
