PlayStation 2 FAT Turquoise Blue – Ratchet & Clank Edition (Japan)

PlayStation 2LimitedJP (NTSC-J)2002

PlayStation 2 Ratchet & Clank Action Pack, blue version, a Toys "R" Us exclusive in Japan, released on December 3, 2002 for the eighth anniversary of the original PlayStation. Console and controller share the same blue shade, which Sony designates with the suffix TB — collectors call it Toy Blue, steel blue or turquoise. The vertical stand is not included. The print run is estimated at between five and ten thousand units, making it the rarest hardware edition in the series.
