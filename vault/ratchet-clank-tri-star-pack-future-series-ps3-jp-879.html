Ratchet & Clank Tri Star Pack (Future series)

PlayStation 3Standard editionJP (NTSC-J)2014

Ginga Saikyō Tri-Star Pack, a Japanese box set released on June 12, 2014. It brings together the Future arc entries released on disc in Japan — Quest for Booty and QForce were download-only there. Not to be confused with the 2012 Gorgeous Pack, which collects the three remastered PlayStation 2 entries.
