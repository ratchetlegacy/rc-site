Ratchet & Clank

PlayStation 2Standard editionUSA (NTSC-U)2002

North American edition of the first game, released on November 4, 2002. Its barcode follows the rule Sony used in North America at the time: the prefix 711719, then the game's serial number, then the check digit — a system that lets you work out one from the other across almost the entire PlayStation 2 catalog.
