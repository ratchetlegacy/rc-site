🧑‍🚀 Ratchet

HERO · LOMBAX

RoleHERO · LOMBAX

Height1.55 m

FamilyKaden (father)

"Let's go, Clank."

A skilled mechanic from planet Veldin and the last known Lombax. Brave, hot-headed and endlessly resourceful, he wields a huge arsenal of wild weapons on his quest to uncover his own origins.

Appears in

Ratchet & Clank (2002), Going Commando, Up Your Arsenal, Deadlocked, Size Matters, Tools of Destruction, Secret Agent Clank, Quest for Booty, A Crack in Time, All 4 One, Full Frontal Assault, Into the Nexus, Ratchet & Clank (2016), Rift Apart, Ranger Rumble

In the games

  • Ratchet & Clank (2002) — In Ratchet & Clank, Ratchet completed his ship but required a robotic ignition system to fly. After recovering a robot that crash-landed in the Kyzil Plateau, the robot presented him an infobot detailing blarg Chairman Drek's plan to destroy planet Novalis for his new planet.
  • Going Commando — At the beginning of Going Commando, celebrated as galactic heroes, Ratchet and Clank both appeared in an interview for Behind the Hero, in which they described their life following Drek's defeat.
  • Up Your Arsenal — Sometime between the events of Going Commando and Up Your Arsenal, Ratchet built a gravimetric warp drive out of blargian scrap metal, and did not test the device. He also starred as Jeeves, Clank's chauffeur, in the series Secret Agent Clank.
  • Deadlocked — After Sasha was appointed mayor of Metropolis, Ratchet was made commander of the Starship Phoenix, with Clank as his first officer and Al as his chief engineer.
  • Going Mobile — In a mobile phone game, Going Mobile, Ratchet and Clank were invited to look at new biomatter converter technology, though the device accidentally transported them to a Secret Agent Clank vid-comic.
  • Clone Home — In Clone Home, Ratchet was contacted by a digital clone of himself who revealed that Gleeman Vox had utilized his digital signature to create a "virus" of billions of clones to overtake the Mega-Net.
  • Size Matters — In Size Matters, wishing to take a much-needed break from heroism, Ratchet took a vacation on Pokitaru with Clank. His break was shortlived, as he met Luna, a technomite robot posing as a schoolgirl writing a report on heroes.
  • Tools of Destruction — While Ratchet was testing the nuclear-powered rocket sled in his home on Kerwan, Qwark sent an urgent message from the Planetary Defense Center asking for help with the Imperial Army's invasion.
  • Secret Agent Clank — A mind control device was planted on Ratchet by Klunk, causing him to steal the Eye of Infinity from the Boltaire Museum, and when filmed, claiming he did it out of revenge for the galaxy's lack of gratitude for his heroism.
  • Quest for Booty — Following a lead from the IRIS Supercomputer, Ratchet and Talwyn traveled to Merdegraw in search of Angstrom Darkwater for more clues about the Zoni and Clank's location.
  • A Crack in Time — Searching for Clank, Ratchet traveled with Captain Qwark to the Breegus System, a region controlled by Dr. Nefarious and his enforcer, Lord Vorselon. Early in his journey, Ratchet encountered Alister Azimuth, a friend of his father's.
  • All 4 One — Ratchet and Clank told Kip Darling and Pepper Fairbanks in an interview that they remained retired, leaving galactic protection to the Polaris Defense Force.
  • Full Frontal Assault — Three months after Qwark lost the next presidential election, he, Ratchet, and Clank resided aboard the Starship Phoenix II.
  • Into the Nexus — Ratchet, alongside Clank, Cronk, and Zephyr, was assigned to the Nebulox Seven to escort Vendra Prog to the Vartax Detention Facility. The mission was abruptly shattered when Neftin Prog and his hired Thugs-4-Less mercenaries attacked, freeing Vendra, who subsequently killed Cronk and Zephyr.
  • Ratchet & Clank (2016) — On Veldin, Ratchet dreams of joining the Galactic Rangers. He meets Clank, a defective warbot who escaped Chairman Drek's factory on Quartu, and the duo earn their place among the Rangers. Together they expose the plan of Drek and Dr. Nefarious to tear apart planets with the Deplanetizer, survive Captain Qwark's betrayal and destroy the weapon, saving the Solana Galaxy.
  • Rift Apart — During a parade in Megalopolis celebrating their careers, Dr. Nefarious steals the Dimensionator and tears reality apart. Separated from Clank, Ratchet lands in a dimension ruled by Emperor Nefarious, where he meets the warbot Kit, while Clank teams up with Rivet, the lombax of that dimension. Ratchet repairs the Dimensionator, reunites with Clank and, alongside Rivet and Kit, defeats Emperor Nefarious.
  • Ranger Rumble — Ratchet mentors the new recruits inside Captain Qwark's combat simulation, alongside Clank, Rivet and Kit, and opposes Qwark's plan to sell the Ranger Rumble franchise to the mercenary firm MMM. He also enters the simulation as a limited-time event hero, armed with the Burst Pistol, a throwable wrench and his OmniWrench, with the RYNO as his Ultimate.
