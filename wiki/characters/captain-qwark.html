🧑‍🚀 Captain Qwark

ALLY · GALACTIC RANGER · COMIC RELIEF

RoleALLY · GALACTIC RANGER · COMIC RELIEF
SpeciesUnknown humanoid
HomeworldKerwan
Height3.20m
First appearanceRatchet & Clank (2002)
WeaponsQwark's jetpack, Plasma-Mitrail, Qwark gadgets, various spacecraft
Voice actorJim Ward

"It's hero time!"

A famous but extremely egotistical superhero whose courage often disappears when danger becomes real. Despite his flaws, Qwark repeatedly proves that he can be a genuine hero when it matters most.

Appears in

Ratchet & Clank (2002), Going Commando, Up Your Arsenal, Deadlocked, Size Matters, Tools of Destruction, Secret Agent Clank, A Crack in Time, All 4 One, Full Frontal Assault, Into the Nexus, Ratchet & Clank (2016), Rift Apart, Ranger Rumble

In the games

  • Ratchet & Clank (2002) — Chairman Drek hired Qwark as the highly paid spokesperson for his new planet. Drek tasked Qwark with stopping Ratchet and Clank, who posed a threat to his plans.
  • Going Commando — Disguised as "Steve McQwark", Qwark sold one million Personal Hygenators to finance his comeback, one of which was purchased by Ratchet. Qwark kept his personal merchandise in his hideout on the Thran Asteroid Belt.
  • Up Your Arsenal — When Dr. Nefarious attacked the Solana Galaxy once more, President Phyronix received a top-secret report on Qwark's whereabouts, as Qwark was the only man to have ever faced Nefarious and beat him.
  • Deadlocked — Qwark did not appear physically in Deadlocked, but delivered a message to Ratchet and Clank over comlink following the destruction of DreadZone Station.
  • Size Matters — Qwark followed Ratchet and Clank to Pokitaru, where they were vacationing, in the hopes of joining in on their next adventure. After Clank questioned if he should instead be with his family, Qwark broke into tears and explained he had been raised by monkeys on Florana.
  • Tools of Destruction — While serving as Chief Volunteer at Kerwan's Planetary Defense Center, Qwark urgently requested help from Ratchet and Clank when the Imperial Army—led by Emperor Tachyon—began attacking Metropolis.
  • Secret Agent Clank — Klunk anonymously sent Qwark a large sum to write an autobiography and have Barney as his biographer. The goal was that Qwark would use his tracking device to follow Clank, in an attempt to steal his glory.
  • A Crack in Time — Around the events of A Crack in Time, Qwark's movies My Blaster Runs Hot and Unicop were advertised on space radio, and Qwark began a press tour. However, a leaked recording of him mistreating a lighting director on the set of My Blaster Runs Hot led many fans to boycott the movie.
  • All 4 One — During Qwark's presidency, several attempts were made on his life. His presidency was tainted by the galaxy being in debt, the city of Luminopolis, Igliak considered a "shambles", and him taking a lavish Meridian City retreat forged from raritanium.
  • Full Frontal Assault — At some point during his time as Galactic President, Qwark spent taxpayer money to have workers at the Hall of Knowledge build a galactic weather control grid into the galaxy's defense network, to make sure the weather was always pleasantly sunny for his presidential visits.
  • Into the Nexus — Qwark had written and published his own memoir entitled Muscles, Memories, and Masks: My Life as a Legend, which was handed out for free at the Intergalactic Museum of History gift shop in Meridian City, Igliak.
  • Ratchet & Clank (2016) — Captain Qwark narrates this retelling of Ratchet and Clank's first adventure. As leader of the Galactic Rangers, he welcomes the duo into the team, then grows jealous of their fame and sides with Chairman Drek before facing them on the Deplanetizer.
  • Rift Apart — Qwark helped organize Megalopolis' Festival of Heroes, which he also hosted alongside Skidd McMarx and Rusty Pete, providing commentary on Ratchet and Clank's life journey so far.
  • Ranger Rumble — Captain Qwark creates the Ranger Rumble, a virtual combat simulation meant to train the next generation of Galactic Rangers, with Ratchet, Clank, Rivet and Kit as mentors. Behind the scenes, he plans to sell the franchise to the mercenary firm MMM.
