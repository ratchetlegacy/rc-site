🧑‍🚀 The Plumber

COMIC RELIEF · PLUMBER · RECURRING CHARACTER

RoleCOMIC RELIEF · PLUMBER · RECURRING CHARACTER
First appearanceRatchet & Clank (2002)
WeaponsWrench, plumbing equipment
Voice actorNeil Flynn
FamilyGary (son)

"Hey, it's me again!"

A mysterious plumber who repeatedly appears in the strangest places throughout the galaxy. Despite his ordinary profession, he often possesses exactly the information or item Ratchet and Clank need to continue their adventure.

Appears in

Ratchet & Clank (2002), Going Commando, Up Your Arsenal, Tools of Destruction, Secret Agent Clank, A Crack in Time, All 4 One, Full Frontal Assault, Into the Nexus, Ratchet & Clank (2016), Rift Apart

In the games

  • Going Commando — During their adventures in the Bogon Galaxy, Ratchet and Clank encountered the Plumber aboard the flying lab on Aranos, where he had been called in to unblock a pipe, a job he accepted as he was paid triple time to complete it, plus travel expenses.
  • Up Your Arsenal — The Plumber later met Ratchet and Clank once again, this time in the sewers of Aquatos. Upon their arrival, he ominously exclaimed that they were "right on schedule" (implying he knew when they would show up).
  • Deadlocked — While Ratchet and Clank competed in DreadZone, the Plumber was called out due to a sump pump emergency in the Rygylian Nebula, meaning he was unable to appear.
  • Size Matters — The Plumber did not physically appear, nor was he mentioned, although he could be seen floating around in Ratchet's Dreamtime.
  • Tools of Destruction — Ratchet and Clank found the Plumber in the lombax testing facility in Outpost L51, Sargasso, in "Investigate the Kerchu Outpost". He had created a "high-speed interplanetary transportation gizmo" (which resembled a toilet).
  • Secret Agent Clank — Ratchet encountered the Plumber on the Prison Planet in the showers, fixing the cold water. He asked Ratchet to give him a hand, though this knocked something loose and angered the nearby prisoners, starting a brawl.
  • A Crack in Time — The Plumber entered Clank's subconscious, mistaking it for Qwark's due to the emptiness. Clank met the Plumber there in "Test Mnemonic Station Iota", seeing the Plumber try to unclog a pipe.
  • All 4 One — The Plumber was called into Magnus by the home office to fill in for another mechanic and fix a broken versa-fuse. There, he found Ratchet, Clank, Captain Qwark, and Dr. Nefarious, and by fixing the fuse, repaired the exit platform that they used to reach Uzo City.
  • Full Frontal Assault — Stuart Zurgo used a hologuise to disguise himself as the Plumber and reach the Starship Phoenix II. The real Plumber had never heard of the Starship Phoenix II, but one morning did meet the Q-Force on Markazia, stranded due to a loose fusion coil on a crashed ship.
  • Into the Nexus — At some point before the game, the Plumber invented the RYNO VII, breaking it up into holo-plans for safekeeping.
  • Rift Apart — During events of Rift Apart, the Plumber took his first ever vacation and entrusted his son, Gary, to watch over the dimensions.
