🧑‍🚀 Clank

HERO · ROBOT

RoleHERO · ROBOT
SpeciesRobot · Zoni
HomeworldQuartu / Great Clock
AgeNew-born
Height0.67 m

WeaponsHeli-Pack, Thruster-Pack, Hydro-Pack, Chronoscepter, various robotic abilities

FamilyOrvus (father), Clank's mother

"Good thinking, Ratchet."

A small, brilliant robot (designation XJ-0461) born by accident on a Blarg factory line. Calm, clever and loyal, he is the brains to Ratchet's brawn — and hides a destiny far greater than his size.

Appears in

Ratchet & Clank (2002), Going Commando, Up Your Arsenal, Deadlocked, Size Matters, Tools of Destruction, Secret Agent Clank, Quest for Booty, A Crack in Time, All 4 One, Full Frontal Assault, Into the Nexus, Ratchet & Clank (2016), Rift Apart, Ranger Rumble

In the games

  • Ratchet & Clank (2002) — Clank comes off the assembly line of Chairman Drek's robot factory on Quartu as a defective warbot. After discovering Drek's plan to build a new Blarg world from pieces of other planets, he escapes, crash-lands on Veldin and teams up with Ratchet. Riding on Ratchet's back, he helps track Drek across the Solana Galaxy and stop the Deplanetizer.
  • Going Commando — Clank appeared with Ratchet in an interview for Behind the Hero, discussing their activities since Drek's defeat. During the interview, Clank's admirer, a female infobot, was hiding behind his chair.
  • Up Your Arsenal — Sometime prior to the events of Up Your Arsenal, Clank became a star in his own holovision show, Secret Agent Clank. The show had many fans, including President Phyronix and Dr. Nefarious.
  • Deadlocked — Clank was appointed first officer of the Starship Phoenix after Sasha Phyronix was appointed mayor of Metropolis, with Ratchet as captain and Al as chief engineer.
  • Going Mobile — In a mobile phone game, Going Mobile, Ratchet and Clank were invited to look at new biomatter converter technology, though the device accidentally transported them to a Secret Agent Clank vid-comic.
  • Clone Home — In Clone Home, Clank coordinated with his own digital clone to stop Gleeman Vox's plan to paralyze the Mega-Net with billions of digital copies.
  • Size Matters — While vacationing with Ratchet on Pokitaru, the duo was interrupted by Luna, a technomite robot posing as a schoolgirl, that asked help for a school project. After, Clank noticed Captain Qwark, and a question by him reminded Qwark he had never known his real parents.
  • Tools of Destruction — While advising Ratchet on the nuclear-powered rocket sled, Kerwan came under attack by Emperor Tachyon, the last cragmite in the universe, whose Imperial Army came to kill Ratchet.
  • Secret Agent Clank — While working for the Agency, the Eye of Infinity jewel was stolen from the Boltaire Museum by Ratchet, mind-controlled by Klunk.
  • Quest for Booty — Clank was taken to the Great Clock, where Dr. Nefarious, deceiving the Zoni into believing he was repairing Clank, spent time attempting to hack into Clank's brain in search of the Orvus Chamber, a central room within the Great Clock.
  • A Crack in Time — After being denied access to the Orvus Chamber by the Zoni, Dr. Nefarious lost patience and unleashed the Hypersonic Brainwave Scrambler on them, inadvertently freeing Clank in the process.
  • All 4 One — In an interview with Kip Darling and Pepper Fairbanks, Clank and Ratchet revealed that they had declined to join President Qwark's hunt for Dr. Nefarious.
  • Full Frontal Assault — Three months after Qwark lost the next presidential election, he, Clank, and Ratchet resided aboard the Starship Phoenix II.
  • Into the Nexus — Clank, who had recently received the Clank Light upgrade from Al, was assigned to the Nebulox Seven with Ratchet, Cronk, and Zephyr, to escort Vendra Prog to the Vartax Detention Facility. Neftin Prog and his hired Thugs-4-Less mercenaries attacked, freeing Vendra, who killed Cronk and Zephyr.
  • Ratchet & Clank (2016) — Clank escapes a Blarg warbot factory on Quartu as a defective unit, carrying a warning about Chairman Drek's plan. Teamed with Ratchet, he joins the Galactic Rangers, helps reveal the true purpose of the Deplanetizer and stands beside Ratchet in the final battle against Dr. Nefarious.
  • Rift Apart — Clank spent the following years repairing the Dimensionator and returned it to its original design.
  • Ranger Rumble — Clank mentors the recruits in Captain Qwark's combat simulation alongside Ratchet, Rivet and Kit. Players who pre-registered for the game received an exclusive Clank Suit skin for the hero Widget.
