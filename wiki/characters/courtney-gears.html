Courtney Gears is an antagonist in Up Your Arsenal, with cameo appearances in Deadlocked and mentions in subsequent entries.

Appears in

Up Your Arsenal, Deadlocked

In the games

  • Up Your Arsenal — Ratchet and Clank first met Courtney Gears during the mission "Win the Tyhrra-Guise". After they won the Annihilation Nation challenge "Grand Prize Bout", Gears presented them with the Tyhrra-Guise as their prize.
  • Deadlocked — Gears first appeared in Deadlocked when Al, a member of Team Darkstar, was seen in his shop dancing to the "Robots of the Galaxy" music video. Startled by Ratchet's entry, Al spat out his coffee and quickly turned the video off.
