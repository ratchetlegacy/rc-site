Captain Qwark creates a combat simulation to train and recruit the next generation of Galactic Rangers. Legendary heroes from across the Ratchet & Clank universe enter the simulation and battle each other in explosive arena matches.

Story

Ratchet & Clank: Ranger Rumble

After years of galactic adventures, Captain Qwark decides that the galaxy needs a new generation of heroes.

Rather than simply recruiting Rangers in the traditional way, Qwark creates what he describes as a revolutionary combat simulation.

The purpose of the simulation is to identify the next generation of Galactic Rangers.

Of course, because the project was created by Captain Qwark, the simulation is considerably less safe than he claims.

Qwark brings together legendary heroes, warriors, robots, aliens and Lombaxes from across the galaxy—and even from different dimensions.

The participants are placed inside simulated combat arenas based on locations from the Ratchet & Clank universe.

Among these locations are places such as Megalopolis and Torren IV.

Each Ranger has their own weapons, gadgets and special abilities.

The recruits must fight against one another while completing different objectives and surviving increasingly chaotic battles.

Some simulations are straightforward battles between Rangers.

Others introduce hostile creatures, bosses and environmental dangers, forcing the competitors to fight both their opponents and the simulation itself.

The simulation includes several different combat scenarios.

In Total Rumble, Rangers fight one another in a chaotic free-for-all.

In Bolt Race, competitors collect bolts while simultaneously dealing with enemies, bosses and rival Rangers.

In Mob Rush, Rangers must defend an arena against waves of enemies and powerful bosses.

Other challenges include Last Stand, where Rangers must survive as the combat zone gradually closes around them, and Blast Ball, a weapon-based sport where teams attempt to score goals with a powerful ball.

As the simulation continues, more Rangers join the program.

Some are familiar faces from the history of the galaxy, while others are completely new characters.

Among the new recruits are characters such as Widget, a Lombax journalist, and other Lombaxes and aliens created specifically for Ranger Rumble.

The simulation therefore becomes more than a simple training exercise.

It becomes a huge meeting point for characters from different parts of the Ratchet & Clank universe.

Even Ratchet and Clank eventually enter the simulation.

Their presence demonstrates just how far Qwark's project has expanded.

However, the simulation is not completely stable.

Qwark's technology occasionally produces strange glitches and unexpected situations.

Rather than shutting everything down, Qwark usually treats these problems as additional opportunities for training.

The Rangers continue competing, improving their equipment and unlocking new abilities.

The ultimate goal remains the same:

prove that you have what it takes to become a Galactic Ranger.

Unlike the traditional Ratchet & Clank adventures, there is no single galaxy-threatening villain to defeat and no definitive final battle.

The story of Ranger Rumble is instead built around Qwark's simulation and its continuing evolution.

New Rangers, weapons, arenas and events can be added to the simulation over time.

The result is essentially a living combat program within the Ratchet & Clank universe, allowing heroes from different eras and dimensions to fight alongside—and against—one another.

And naturally, Captain Qwark remains convinced that he has created the greatest Ranger training program in the history of the galaxy.

Whether anyone should actually trust Qwark with a simulation containing this many explosives is another question entirely.

Did you know?

Ranger Rumble is the first Ratchet & Clank game designed specifically as a mobile multiplayer hero shooter. The game also introduces brand-new Lombax characters created in collaboration with Insomniac Games. After soft launches in Asia and Northern Europe from late 2025, then in France in March 2026, Canada in May and the United Kingdom in August, it launched worldwide on 5 October 2026; worldwide pre-registration had opened in July. Development ended on 6 November 2026 with a final update that removed in-app purchases and made the Super Pass free for everyone. The game leaves the App Store and Google Play on 21 November 2026 and its servers shut down on 1 May 2027: as an online-only game, it no longer loads after that date. Ratchet Legacy is archiving it in the Ranger Rumble Save Project.

Modes

  • Bolt Race (3 min · 6 players · free-for-all) — Collect bolts from crates and enemies. A boss appears in the middle of the map after 90 seconds and drops bolts with every hit. First player to 300 bolts wins.
    Icon: https://res.cloudinary.com/dmfq8hkvu/image/upload/Wiki/RangerRumble/rr-mode-bolt-race.webp · Maps: Qwark Tower, Transit Hub, Dismantling Centre, Little Junktown, Biting Swamp, Black Market
  • Total Rumble (3 min · 6 players · free-for-all) — Deathmatch: the first player to 8 knockouts wins. Shield and damage power-ups (+50% damage) appear on the map.
    Icon: https://res.cloudinary.com/dmfq8hkvu/image/upload/Wiki/RangerRumble/rr-mode-total-rumble.webp · Maps: Shopping District, Train Station, The Foundry, Molonoth Gulch, Gelatonium Factory, Pirate Hideout
  • Team Total Rumble (3 v 3) — Team version of Total Rumble: the two teams start on opposite sides and the first team to 10 knockouts wins.
    Icon: https://res.cloudinary.com/dmfq8hkvu/image/upload/Wiki/RangerRumble/rr-mode-team-total-rumble.webp · Maps: Shopping District, Train Station, The Foundry, Molonoth Gulch, Gelatonium Factory, Pirate Hideout
  • Last Stand (6 players · battle royale) — No respawns. A forcefield shrinks the arena over time and overhealth pickups make you tougher. The last Ranger standing wins.
    Icon: https://res.cloudinary.com/dmfq8hkvu/image/upload/Wiki/RangerRumble/rr-mode-last-stand.webp · Maps: Qwark Tower, Transit Hub, Dismantling Centre, Little Junktown, Biting Swamp, Black Market
  • Blast Ball (3 min · 3 v 3) — A football match with guns: shoot the ball to move it and score two goals. A draw goes to one minute of overtime, first goal wins.
    Icon: https://res.cloudinary.com/dmfq8hkvu/image/upload/Wiki/RangerRumble/rr-mode-blast-ball.webp · Maps: Grand Stadium, Clash of the Titans, Sunken Field
  • Zone Control (4 min · 2 teams) — Capture zones by standing in them, one active zone at a time. Contested zones stop progressing. Two captured zones guarantee the win.
    Icon: https://res.cloudinary.com/dmfq8hkvu/image/upload/Wiki/RangerRumble/rr-mode-zone-control.webp · Maps: Qwark Tower, Train Station, Transit Hub, Biting Swamp
  • Mob Rush (Solo · PvE) — Defend Captain Qwark's vault against waves of enemies (3 to 9 waves per stage) across 17 stages on Corson V, Torren IV and Sargasso.
    Icon: https://res.cloudinary.com/dmfq8hkvu/image/upload/Wiki/RangerRumble/rr-mode-mob-rush.webp · Maps: Rangers Promenade, Raritanium Excavation, Grunthor Den

Economy

  • Qredit — Main currency, used with Hero Lorbs to level up heroes.
    Icon: https://res.cloudinary.com/dmfq8hkvu/image/upload/Wiki/RangerRumble/rr-cur-qredit.webp
  • Hero Lorb — Hero-specific upgrade item; each hero has his own Lorb type.
    Icon: https://res.cloudinary.com/dmfq8hkvu/image/upload/Wiki/RangerRumble/rr-cur-hero-lorb.webp
  • Power Core — Unlocks hero Talents (1 Core at level 3, 3 Cores at level 6).
    Icon: https://res.cloudinary.com/dmfq8hkvu/image/upload/Wiki/RangerRumble/rr-cur-power-core.webp
  • Fashionium — Spent on cosmetic skins.
    Icon: https://res.cloudinary.com/dmfq8hkvu/image/upload/Wiki/RangerRumble/rr-cur-fashionium.webp
  • Raritanium — Premium currency: resources, heroes and Qwest refreshes.
    Icon: https://res.cloudinary.com/dmfq8hkvu/image/upload/Wiki/RangerRumble/rr-cur-raritanium.webp
  • Reebo — Loot box won by placing 1st to 3rd; contains Qredits, Raritanium, Fashionium, Power Cores or Hero Lorbs. Refreshed hourly.
    Icon: https://res.cloudinary.com/dmfq8hkvu/image/upload/Wiki/RangerRumble/rr-cur-reebo.webp

Progression

  • Ranger Road: the main progression track, advanced with Hero Medals; milestones unlock modes, heroes and rewards.
  • Hero Medals: earned each match depending on placement and winstreak; every 100 medals unlocks a hero reward, with a free skin at tier 10.
  • Qwests: daily and seasonal objectives (deal damage, play games, use your Ultimate…). Daily Qwests can be refreshed for 10 Raritanium.
