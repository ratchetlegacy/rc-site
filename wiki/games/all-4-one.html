🎮 All 4 One

PlayStation 3 · 2011

PlatformPlayStation 3
Year2011
PlayableRatchet, Clank, Captain Qwark, Dr. Nefarious
Release18 October 2011 (NA) · 19 October 2011 (EU)
DeveloperInsomniac Games
ProgressionWeapons are shared and level up as the team uses them.

A four-player co-op spin-off where Ratchet, Clank, Qwark and even Dr. Nefarious must team up.

Story

The story begins sometime after the events of A Crack in Time.

Ratchet and Clank have once again saved the galaxy, but Ratchet has decided that it is time to retire from his life as a hero.

Tired of constantly putting his life at risk, Ratchet wants to settle down and live a quieter life with Clank.

However, their peaceful retirement does not last long.

The two are invited to Luminopolis, where the citizens are preparing to honor Ratchet as a legendary hero.

Ratchet is uncomfortable with the attention, but Clank encourages him to accept the honor.

The ceremony is interrupted when the former villain Doctor Nefarious suddenly appears.

Nefarious claims that he has changed and now wants to become a hero.

Ratchet immediately suspects that something is wrong.

Before anyone can stop him, Nefarious activates a mysterious machine hidden beneath the city.

The machine was designed to summon an unknown creature known as the Creature Collector.

Instead, the machine malfunctions.

A gigantic creature emerges from another dimension and begins consuming everything around it.

The creature is eventually revealed to be the Giant Creature, an enormous being that feeds on energy and matter.

The situation quickly gets out of control.

Ratchet, Clank, Nefarious, and Captain Qwark are caught in the chaos and are forced to work together to survive.

The four heroes are eventually transported to a strange region of space known as the Deadgrove.

They soon discover that the creature's arrival was not an accident.

The mysterious machine has created a dimensional imbalance, and the only way to stop the creature is to understand where it came from.

The unlikely group begins investigating the surrounding planets.

Ratchet is frustrated by having to work alongside Nefarious.

Qwark, meanwhile, is delighted to have another opportunity to play the hero.

Clank tries to keep everyone focused on the mission.

Nefarious constantly claims that he has changed, but Ratchet remains convinced that the doctor is planning something.

As the group travels through the galaxy, they encounter several strange civilizations and environments affected by the dimensional disturbance.

They eventually discover that the creature is connected to an ancient race known as the Z'Grute.

The Z'Grute are responsible for creating the technology that allowed the creature to enter their dimension.

The group also discovers that the dimensional energy released by the machine is spreading.

If they cannot stop it, entire worlds could be consumed.

To make matters worse, Nefarious begins secretly manipulating the situation.

He claims that the only way to stop the creature is to collect several powerful energy sources scattered throughout the region.

Ratchet and the others reluctantly agree to help him.

The four heroes therefore travel from planet to planet, fighting monsters, solving puzzles, and collecting the components needed to repair the dimensional device.

Their journey eventually brings them into conflict with the Creature, which continues growing stronger by absorbing energy.

Ratchet begins realizing that their real problem is not simply the creature itself.

The creature is being controlled.

Someone is using it.

Eventually, the heroes discover the truth.

Nefarious has been manipulating events from the beginning.

His supposed desire to become a hero was nothing more than another scheme.

He intends to use the creature's immense power for himself.

Nefarious wants to absorb the energy generated by the dimensional disturbance and become powerful enough to reshape the universe according to his own wishes.

Ratchet confronts him.

Nefarious admits that he never truly intended to become a hero.

He simply needed Ratchet, Clank, and Qwark to help him activate the technology and reach the creature.

The four heroes finally turn against Nefarious.

However, Nefarious manages to escape and takes control of the dimensional machine.

The machine begins destabilizing the entire region.

The creature grows even larger and starts consuming entire areas of space.

Ratchet realizes that they cannot defeat it through brute force.

They must shut down the machine controlling it.

Ratchet, Clank, Qwark, and even Nefarious's former allies work together to reach the machine.

During the final confrontation, Nefarious attempts to absorb the creature's energy.

But the plan goes wrong.

The enormous amount of energy overwhelms him.

The creature turns against Nefarious and begins absorbing him instead.

Ratchet and Clank take advantage of the opportunity and destroy the machine.

The dimensional disturbance begins to collapse.

The creature is finally defeated and the worlds affected by the crisis are saved.

Nefarious, however, survives.

The energy surge sends him flying through space once again.

Ratchet, Clank, and Qwark return to Luminopolis as heroes.

The citizens celebrate their victory.

Ratchet realizes that, despite everything, being a hero is still part of who he is.

He may have wanted to retire, but when the galaxy needs him, he cannot simply stand aside.

Clank is happy to see his friend embrace his role once again.

Qwark, naturally, takes full credit for the victory.

And Nefarious is once again left drifting through space, having failed yet another attempt to conquer the universe.

The adventure ends with Ratchet, Clank, and Qwark continuing their lives as heroes.

Despite their constant disagreements, the unlikely team has proven that sometimes the galaxy's greatest victories come from the most unlikely group of heroes.

Ratchet, Clank, Qwark... and even Nefarious.

Watching arch-enemies Qwark and Nefarious forced to cooperate is half the fun.

Modes

  • Co-op story (1–4 players) — Ratchet, Clank, Qwark and even Dr. Nefarious must work together after being captured by the Z'Grute collector.

Economy

  • Bolts — Main currency, dropped by enemies and crates; spent at Gadgetron vendors on weapons, gadgets and ammo.
