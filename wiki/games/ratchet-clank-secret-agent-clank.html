🎮 Ratchet & Clank: Secret Agent Clank

PlayStation Portable / PlayStation 2 · 2008

PlatformPlayStation Portable / PlayStation 2
Year2008
PlayableClank, Ratchet, Captain Qwark
Release17 June 2008 (NA) · 11 July 2008 (EU)
DeveloperHigh Impact Games (PSP) · Sanzaru Games (PS2)

When Ratchet is falsely accused of stealing the legendary Eye of Infinity, he is sent to a maximum-security prison. Convinced that his friend is innocent, Clank takes on his famous Secret Agent persona and begins investigating the theft. His investigation leads him across the galaxy and eventually reveals a much more dangerous plan involving his evil duplicate, Klunk.

Story

The story begins at the Boltaire Museum, where the galaxy's most precious gemstone, the legendary Eye of Infinity, is kept under extremely heavy security.

The Eye is considered the largest and most pristine gem in the known universe.

During the night, the museum's security systems are breached.

Clank is shocked when he witnesses the thief escaping.

Even more shocking is the identity of the culprit.

It appears to be Ratchet.

Ratchet is immediately arrested and accused of stealing the Eye of Infinity.

He is sent to a maximum-security prison filled with criminals he has encountered during his previous adventures.

Ratchet insists that he is innocent, but the evidence against him appears overwhelming.

Clank refuses to believe that his best friend could have committed such a crime.

Determined to prove Ratchet's innocence, Clank activates his Secret Agent Clank persona and begins investigating the theft.

His first clue leads him back to the Boltaire Museum.

Clank discovers evidence pointing toward Asyanica and begins following the trail of the stolen Eye.

Meanwhile, Ratchet is having a much less glamorous adventure.

Inside the prison, he is surrounded by former enemies who are more than happy to take revenge on the hero who helped put them behind bars.

Ratchet is forced to fight his way through the prison while Clank continues his investigation outside.

Clank eventually reaches the rooftops of Asyanica and tracks down a criminal known as Number Woo.

Woo admits that he had possession of the Eye of Infinity, but reveals that he did not keep it.

He had transferred the gem to Countess Ivana Lottabolts on the frozen planet of Glaciara.

Clank travels to Glaciara and confronts the Countess.

Rather than simply handing over the Eye, Ivana challenges Clank to a dangerous dance of death, forcing him to navigate a series of traps.

Clank survives the challenge.

The Countess finally reveals that the Eye has already been passed to another criminal: a mysterious figure known only as the Kingpin.

The Kingpin is currently operating on Rionosis.

Clank follows the trail to Rionosis.

However, the Kingpin manages to escape before Clank can capture him.

Clank pursues him through the city and eventually discovers that the Eye has been transported to Le Paradis des Tricheurs Casino.

At the same time, Captain Qwark is following Clank around the galaxy.

Qwark is supposedly writing his autobiography and has brought along a reporter robot named B.A.R.N.E.Y.

Naturally, Qwark cannot resist turning Clank's real missions into stories about his own supposed heroism.

Every dangerous situation becomes, in Qwark's version, another legendary adventure in which he personally saved the galaxy.

Back at the prison, Ratchet continues fighting his former enemies.

He eventually encounters Slim Cognito, the shady weapons dealer he has met before.

Slim offers Ratchet a way to obtain the information Clank needs.

Meanwhile, Clank infiltrates the casino.

The Eye of Infinity is being used as part of a high-stakes game.

Clank enters a rigged poker game in order to obtain the gem.

Against all odds, he manages to win.

But his victory is short-lived.

The Eye has already been moved again.

This time, it has been taken to the Venantonio Laboratories.

Clank follows the trail and discovers that a scientist has been forced to work for the Kingpin.

The scientist has been ordered to construct a powerful laser system.

The scientist eventually helps Clank escape the laboratory.

Clank then travels toward Fort Sprocket, where he discovers that the Kingpin has infiltrated the Galactic Bolt Reserve.

The facility has been taken over by mysterious duplicates.

Clank realizes that the Kingpin has access to technology capable of creating copies of individuals.

He fights his way through the facility and eventually reaches the vault.

But the Kingpin has already escaped.

Clank follows him to a spaceship graveyard.

There, the mysterious Kingpin finally reveals his true identity.

He is Klunk.

Klunk is Clank's evil duplicate, originally created by Dr. Nefarious during the events of Up Your Arsenal.

Klunk has been manipulating events from the shadows.

He was responsible for framing Ratchet.

Before the theft, Klunk used a mind-control helmet to force Ratchet to steal the Eye of Infinity.

He then arranged for the evidence to point directly toward Ratchet.

His plan is far more ambitious than simply stealing a valuable gem.

Klunk has built a gigantic laser system capable of destroying every planet in the galaxy.

The Eye of Infinity is being used as the laser's refractor.

Klunk's plan is to make Ratchet appear responsible for the destruction of the galaxy.

Then, while pretending to be Clank, Klunk would supposedly stop the catastrophe.

He would become a hero in the eyes of the galaxy.

Ratchet would remain imprisoned as the villain who supposedly tried to destroy everything.

Klunk would then replace the real Clank permanently.

Clank realizes that his evil duplicate has carefully planned every step.

But Klunk has one final surprise.

Even if Clank defeats him, the laser has already been placed on a countdown.

Klunk destroys the control mechanism, ensuring that the weapon will continue firing even if he is defeated.

Clank must therefore defeat Klunk and find another way to stop the laser.

The two robots engage in a final battle inside Klunk's underwater base on Hydrano.

Clank fights through Klunk's forces and eventually defeats his evil duplicate.

But the laser is still active.

The Eye of Infinity is positioned to trigger a devastating attack capable of destroying the galaxy.

Fortunately, Clank is not alone.

Captain Qwark arrives to help.

Using the available technology, Qwark reaches the satellite and removes the Eye of Infinity from the laser.

The weapon is disabled before it can destroy the galaxy.

Klunk's entire plan collapses.

Ratchet is finally proven innocent.

The investigation reveals that Ratchet had been controlled by Klunk and never intentionally stole the Eye.

The Agency also discovers that B.A.R.N.E.Y., Qwark's biographer, had secretly been working for Klunk.

He had been hired to follow Qwark because Klunk knew that Qwark would naturally follow Clank around the galaxy while recording his supposed heroic adventures.

Ratchet is given a full pardon.

The Eye of Infinity is returned to the Boltaire Museum.

Klunk, meanwhile, is taken into custody.

Instead of being destroyed, he is eventually placed under Ratchet and Clank's supervision.

Ratchet decides to give his evil duplicate a much less dignified punishment.

Klunk is transformed into a vacuum cleaner and becomes part of Ratchet and Clank's apartment.

The story ends with Ratchet and Clank back home in Megapolis.

Everything appears to be back to normal.

But as the camera focuses on the vacuum cleaner, Klunk's eyes suddenly activate.

His fate may not be quite as final as everyone thought.

The adventure of Secret Agent Clank comes to an end.

Of course, none of this technically happened exactly as shown.

The entire adventure is presented as an episode of the fictional Secret Agent Clank holo-vision series, with Clank playing the heroic secret agent, Ratchet serving as the wrongly accused hero, and Qwark exaggerating his own involvement for his autobiography.

Nevertheless, the events and characters featured in the story are part of the Ratchet & Clank universe's fictional media history.

Did you know?

Secret Agent Clank is the first Ratchet & Clank game in which Clank is the main playable character. However, the adventure is actually an episode of the fictional holo-vision series Secret Agent Clank within the Ratchet & Clank universe. Captain Qwark also gets his own playable sections, where he wildly exaggerates his supposed heroic achievements while writing his autobiography.

Modes

  • Story (Solo · PSP / PS2) — Clank goes undercover as a secret agent to clear Ratchet, who has been jailed for a theft he did not commit.
  • Ratchet in prison (Arena) — Ratchet fights in prison arenas.
  • Qwark stories (Variations) — Captain Qwark tells his own (very embellished) version of events.
  • Gadgebots (Variations) — Clank commands his Gadgebots to solve puzzles.

Economy

  • Bolts — Main currency, dropped by enemies and crates; spent at Gadgetron vendors on weapons, gadgets and ammo.
