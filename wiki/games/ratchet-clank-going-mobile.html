🎮 Ratchet & Clank: Going Mobile

Mobile phones (J2ME) · 2005

PlatformMobile phones (J2ME)
Year2005
PlayableRatchet, Clank
Release2 November 2005 (NA)
DeveloperHandheld Games
LanguagesEnglish, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese (some versions)
PublisherSony Pictures Mobile
CharactersRatchet, Clank, Big Al, Maximillian, Ace Hardlight (posters)

Ratchet and Clank are accidentally transported into a digital Secret Agent Clank vid-comic. Trapped inside the mobile device, they must recover a mysterious device called the MCGuFIN and find a way back to the real world.

Story

Ratchet and Clank are invited to test a new piece of technology known as the Bio Matter Converter.

The device is designed to allow matter to be transported between the physical and digital worlds.

However, something goes wrong.

The machine activates unexpectedly and transports Ratchet and Clank directly into a digital world contained inside a mobile phone.

The two heroes soon discover that they have been trapped inside a Secret Agent Clank vid-comic.

Their surroundings are completely different from the real galaxy.

They must fight their way through digital environments filled with security systems, Megacorp forces and various enemies.

Ratchet and Clank eventually discover an Infolink, allowing them to communicate with the outside world.

They use it to access different parts of the phone's operating system and eventually contact Big Al.

Al explains that there may be a way to get them out.

He tells Ratchet and Clank about a device called the MCGuFIN — Macro Corporeal Geo Fragmentation Ion Negator.

The MCGuFIN is capable of transporting objects between the digital and physical worlds.

Fortunately, Al owns one.

Unfortunately, there is a problem.

The MCGuFIN has been broken into several pieces, with each piece scattered throughout different parts of the digital environment.

Ratchet and Clank therefore begin a long journey through the various virtual areas and vid-comics contained within the phone.

They recover the pieces one by one while fighting security robots, Chickenbots, turrets and other enemies.

Eventually, the heroes manage to reconstruct the MCGuFIN.

But before they can use it, they discover another problem.

Maximillian, the villain from the Secret Agent Clank series, has also discovered a way to escape the digital world.

Maximillian intends to use the technology to reach the physical world and cause chaos.

Ratchet and Clank track him down.

They discover that Maximillian has already obtained the information necessary to escape.

A final confrontation takes place inside his heavily defended area.

Ratchet and Clank defeat Maximillian and destroy his security cannon.

With Maximillian defeated, the heroes finally have access to the technology they need.

They activate the MCGuFIN.

The device begins transporting them back toward the physical world.

The adventure ends as Ratchet and Clank are finally able to leave the digital world, although their exact destination is left somewhat mysterious.

Their strange journey inside the mobile phone is over.

Did you know?

Going Mobile was made by Handheld Games without any direct involvement from Insomniac. Its key plot device, the MCGuFIN, is a pun on the storytelling term "McGuffin".

Weapons

  • Wrench — Used automatically near an opponent or crate. Uses no ammo.
  • Lancer — Fires weak rapid bursts.
  • Gravity Bomb — Light trajectory with a large area-of-effect explosion.
  • Mini Rocket — Straight shot with a small area-of-effect explosion.
  • Defragmenter — Powerful shot that hits all targets, weaker at long range.
  • Circuit Jammer — Rapid, weaker bursts that home in on enemies; also shorts out some security devices. Given by the story.
  • Boar-Zooka — Turns enemies into pork. Won in the Arena.
  • R.Y.N.O. — Powerful homing shots with a small explosion. Unlocked by collecting the 9 titanium bolts.

Controls

  • Up / 2: jump, glide, grapple
  • Left / 4 and Right / 6: move
  • 1 and 3: jump left / right
  • Down / 8: drop through a platform
  • OK / 5: fire, or wrench near an enemy, door bolt or crate
  • *: wrench
  • #: change weapon

Collectibles

  • 9 titanium bolts (unlock the R.Y.N.O.)
  • 6 pieces of the MCGuFIN
  • Bolt, nanotech and Gadgetron crates
