🎮 Quest for Booty

PlayStation 3 · 2008

PlatformPlayStation 3
Year2008
PlayableRatchet
Release21 August 2008 (PlayStation Network)
DeveloperInsomniac Games

A shorter chapter bridging Tools of Destruction and A Crack in Time. Ratchet searches for a way to rescue Clank.

Story

One year has passed since Ratchet and Clank defeated Emperor Tachyon and saved the Polaris Galaxy.

But their victory came at a terrible cost.

At the end of their previous adventure, the mysterious Zoni appeared and abducted Clank, claiming that he was ready to discover his true purpose.

Since then, Ratchet has been desperately searching for his best friend.

Unable to accept Clank's disappearance, Ratchet and Talwyn Apogee turn to the IRIS Supercomputer, hoping that its immense database can provide them with a clue.

IRIS eventually identifies someone who may know how to contact the Zoni: Captain Angstrom Darkwater, a legendary space pirate who once possessed an object capable of communicating with their dimension.

The trail leads Ratchet and Talwyn to the planet Merdegraw, in the Drogol Sector, where a fleet of space pirates is sailing across the Azorean Sea.

Believing that Darkwater may still be somewhere among the pirates, Ratchet and Talwyn sneak aboard one of their ships.

However, they are quickly discovered and captured.

The pirates' leader, Sprocket, reveals the truth: Captain Darkwater has been dead for years.

The two heroes are sentenced to be executed.

Just before they can be killed, Rusty Pete appears.

Pete is carrying the severed head of Captain Romulus Slag, whom Ratchet defeated during the events surrounding Emperor Tachyon.

Pete disguises himself as Slag by holding his head in front of his own body and orders the pirates not to execute Ratchet and Talwyn.

Instead, he convinces Sprocket to send them to Hoolefar Island, claiming that the two Lombax-related travelers may be looking for something connected to Darkwater.

Ratchet and Talwyn are fired from a cannon and land on the island.

As Ratchet recovers from the impact, he suddenly sees something impossible.

For a brief moment, he sees Clank, surrounded by a strange blue energy field.

Ratchet tries to reach him, but Clank disappears before he can get close.

Convinced that the vision must have been a clue from the Zoni, Ratchet continues searching.

On Hoolefar Island, they meet Mayor Barnabas Worley, the leader of the local Hoolefoid population.

Worley explains that the island has been experiencing serious problems.

Its supply beacon has stopped working, cutting the inhabitants off from the outside world.

Ratchet agrees to help.

He repairs several wind turbines scattered across the island and eventually restores power to the beacon.

To complete the repairs, Ratchet needs a special component called a VersaBolt, which he obtains from a mysterious Smuggler.

Once the beacon is operational again, Mayor Worley tells Ratchet about one of Darkwater's greatest creations: the Obsidian Eye.

The Obsidian Eye is an enormous Lorentzian telescope capable of communicating with the Zoni's dimension.

If Ratchet can activate it, he may finally be able to locate Clank.

There is only one problem.

The Obsidian Eye is missing its power source.

Darkwater had removed the Fulcrum Star, an ancient artifact given to him by the Zoni, and hidden it somewhere on the planet.

Ratchet realizes that finding the Fulcrum Star may be the only way to locate Clank.

Rusty Pete eventually joins Ratchet and Talwyn and reveals that Darkwater left behind a map leading to the location of the Star.

Using the map and the remains of Darkwater's old equipment, the group travels to the pirate captain's tomb in the Morrow Caverns.

Deep inside the caves, they discover Darkwater's old ship.

But something is very wrong.

Pete connects the head of Captain Slag to Darkwater's body.

The result is a terrifying resurrection.

Slag's body is now inhabited by the spirit of Darkwater.

The resurrected pirate captain takes control of his former ghost crew, bringing the undead pirates back to life.

Darkwater and Slag, now sharing the same body, set out to reclaim their old power and attack Hoolefar Island.

Ratchet barely escapes the caves and returns to the island.

The pirates launch an assault, forcing Ratchet to defend the Hoolefoids.

After a fierce battle, Ratchet and Talwyn manage to repel the attack.

However, Darkwater and Slag are still searching for the Fulcrum Star.

With the help of the Smuggler, Ratchet follows another clue leading to Darkwater Cove, where the legendary pirate hid his treasure.

The entrance to the treasure vault is protected by a security system that only recognizes Darkwater.

Ratchet manages to trick the system into believing that he is the pirate captain and gains access to the vault.

Inside, he finally discovers the Fulcrum Star.

But before he can retrieve it, a trapdoor opens beneath him.

Ratchet falls into the caves below.

Darkwater and Slag arrive moments later.

They seize the Fulcrum Star and capture Talwyn.

Ratchet is left trapped underground while the pirates escape.

With the help of the Smuggler, Ratchet manages to escape and pursues the pirates across the Azorean Sea.

He reaches their fleet and launches a rescue mission to save Talwyn.

After freeing her, Ratchet finally confronts Slag and Darkwater.

The battle takes place across several pirate ships and eventually ends with Ratchet defeating the resurrected pirate.

Slag and Darkwater are thrown into the sea.

Rusty Pete jumps after them in an attempt to recover his former captain, allowing Ratchet to retrieve the Fulcrum Star.

The curse is finally broken.

With the Star in his possession, Ratchet and Talwyn return to Hoolefar Island.

Ratchet places the Fulcrum Star inside the Obsidian Eye and activates the ancient machine.

The telescope comes to life.

For the first time, Ratchet is finally able to see where Clank has gone.

The Obsidian Eye reveals Clank surrounded by several Zoni in a strange dimension.

But something is wrong.

Clank appears damaged and confused.

He briefly awakens and speaks, but his words make no sense.

Ratchet realizes that Clank is not simply being held prisoner.

The Zoni have taken him somewhere because something has happened to him.

The Zoni then reveal that they have found someone capable of repairing Clank.

A doctor.

Ratchet watches the image carefully.

Then he recognizes the figure approaching the Zoni.

It is Doctor Nefarious.

Ratchet is stunned.

Nefarious was one of his most dangerous enemies and was believed to have been stranded on an asteroid following the events of Ratchet & Clank 3.

Yet somehow, the Zoni have brought him into their dimension.

And now they want him to repair Clank.

Ratchet immediately realizes that he cannot allow Nefarious to be alone with his best friend.

He and Talwyn therefore set a new course toward the Breegus Nebula, determined to rescue Clank before Nefarious can discover the secrets hidden inside him.

Meanwhile, far away in the Azorean Sea, Rusty Pete and Slag are still drifting helplessly on the water.

Pete continues telling Slag the story of everything that has happened.

Slag, however, is less interested in the story than in getting off the piece of wreckage they are stranded on.

He orders Pete to start rowing toward the nearest island.

Ratchet's search for Clank continues.

But he does not yet know that Nefarious has his own plans for the mysterious robot.

And the answers hidden inside Clank's memory will soon reveal the truth about the Zoni, the Great Clock, and Clank's true purpose.

Did you know?

At about a third the length of a full game, it was an early bite-sized digital release.
