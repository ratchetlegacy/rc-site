🎮 Tools of Destruction

PlayStation 3 · 2007

PlatformPlayStation 3
Year2007
PlayableRatchet, Clank
Release23 October 2007 (NA) · 9 November 2007 (EU)
DeveloperInsomniac Games

The Future saga begins. Pursued by Emperor Tachyon, Ratchet flees Solana and starts to uncover the truth about the vanished Lombax race.

Story

The story begins on Kerwan, in the bustling city of Metropolis. Ratchet and Clank are enjoying a relatively peaceful life and are working on one of Ratchet's latest inventions: a nuclear-powered rocket sled.

However, their peaceful day is suddenly interrupted when Clank receives a distress call from Captain Qwark, who is stationed at the Planetary Defense Center.

Before Ratchet and Clank can reach him, Metropolis comes under attack.

An enormous Imperial fleet descends upon the city. The attackers are soldiers of the Imperial Army, an army led by a mysterious ruler known as Emperor Percival Tachyon.

But unlike a normal invasion, the Imperial forces appear to have a very specific objective: Ratchet himself.

Ratchet and Clank eventually come face-to-face with Tachyon, who reveals that he has been searching for the last remaining Lombax in the universe.

Tachyon offers Ratchet a deal: if he surrenders, the Imperial Army will spare Metropolis.

Ratchet refuses to trust him and instead manages to escape with Clank by stealing one of Tachyon's own warships.

The ship's autopilot takes control and puts the two heroes into cryosleep.

When they awaken, they discover that they have been taken far from Solana, into the Polaris Galaxy, a region of space that neither of them knows.

During their journey, Clank begins encountering strange, mysterious creatures known as the Zoni.

The Zoni are invisible to Ratchet and most other beings, but they communicate with Clank and appear to be guiding him toward an unknown objective.

Following their instructions, Ratchet and Clank reach the Hall of Knowledge, where they learn more about the history of the Polaris Galaxy.

Their journey eventually takes them to Fastoon.

Ratchet immediately realizes that something about the planet feels strangely familiar.

Fastoon is an abandoned world and, more importantly, it was once the home planet of the Lombaxes.

Ratchet is stunned.

For the first time, he realizes that the Lombaxes were not simply a species that had disappeared without a trace. They had actually lived here, in the Polaris Galaxy.

The planet is now little more than ruins.

While exploring the remains of the Lombax civilization, Ratchet and Clank discover an old spacecraft named Aphelion.

After repairing the ship, Aphelion reveals information about what happened to Fastoon and the Lombaxes.

The trail eventually leads them to Captain Qwark, who has apparently decided to cooperate with Emperor Tachyon.

Ratchet and Clank track Qwark to the Imperial Fight Festival on Mukow.

There, they discover that Qwark has apparently surrendered to Tachyon and is being used as propaganda for the Imperial Army.

However, Qwark secretly gives Ratchet an InfoBot containing extremely important information.

Tachyon is searching the entire Polaris Galaxy for something called the "Lombax Secret."

Whatever this secret is, Tachyon believes it could allow him to accomplish his ultimate objective.

The clues point toward the Apogee Space Station, located in the Nundac Asteroid Ring.

Ratchet and Clank travel there and meet Talwyn Apogee, the daughter of the famous Lombax explorer Max Apogee.

Talwyn has spent years investigating her father's disappearance and studying Lombax technology.

She joins Ratchet and Clank in their search.

Alongside Talwyn are her two eccentric warbots, Cronk and Zephyr.

Together, the group begins following the clues left behind by Max Apogee.

Their first major breakthrough comes when they recover an ancient Lombax artifact from the space pirates.

When Talwyn attempts to activate it, nothing happens.

But when Ratchet touches it, the artifact reacts.

A three-dimensional map appears, pointing toward Rykan V.

This confirms that Ratchet himself has some kind of connection to the ancient Lombax technology.

The group travels to Rykan V, but the planet is heavily guarded by Tachyon's forces.

After fighting their way through the Imperial blockade, Ratchet and his allies discover an ancient Center for Advanced Lombax Research.

Inside, they find a recording explaining the true nature of the Lombax Secret.

It is not a weapon designed to destroy the Cragmites, as they had initially believed.

The Lombaxes created a device called the Dimensionator.

The Dimensionator is an incredibly powerful helmet capable of opening portals between dimensions.

During the ancient Great War, the Lombaxes used the Dimensionator to banish the Cragmites to another dimension, ending their empire.

This discovery changes everything.

Ratchet now realizes that Tachyon is not simply hunting him because he is a Lombax.

He wants the Dimensionator.

With it, Tachyon could bring the Cragmites back and restore his species.

The group continues searching for the device while Clank investigates the mysterious Zoni.

During one of these encounters, the Zoni show Clank a disturbing vision of Talwyn imprisoned on Zordoom.

Ratchet and Clank immediately set out to rescue her.

At the same time, Ratchet begins learning the truth about Tachyon's origins.

Tachyon was not originally raised as a conqueror.

Long before his rise to power, he was discovered as a frozen Cragmite egg by Lombax miners.

The Lombaxes took him in and raised him on Fastoon.

For years, Tachyon lived among the very people he would eventually try to destroy.

But when Tachyon discovered the truth about his species and learned about the Great War, he became obsessed with revenge.

He eventually built an army, using Lombax technology that he had learned from them, and turned against his adopted people.

His invasion of Fastoon forced the Lombaxes to flee their own world.

Ratchet and Clank eventually discover that the Dimensionator is located on Jasindu.

However, they are not the only ones looking for it.

The space pirates, led by Captain Romulus Slag, are also after the device.

After battling through Slag's forces, Ratchet finally reaches the Dimensionator.

But just as he obtains it, Slag and his pirates arrive and steal it.

Ratchet and Clank pursue them across the galaxy.

Their pursuit eventually leads to a confrontation with Captain Slag.

After defeating him, Ratchet recovers the Dimensionator.

For a brief moment, it seems that the heroes have finally won.

But then Captain Qwark appears.

Qwark takes the Dimensionator from Ratchet, claiming that it is far too dangerous for anyone to possess.

He decides to destroy it by throwing it into a black hole.

Unfortunately, Qwark's escape pod does not take him toward a black hole.

Its automated navigation system sends him directly toward Reepor, the ancient homeworld of the Cragmites.

This is exactly what Tachyon needs.

Tachyon's forces capture Qwark and take the Dimensionator.

Ratchet, Clank, Talwyn, Cronk, and Zephyr arrive too late.

Tachyon activates the Dimensionator.

A portal opens between dimensions.

Through it, Tachyon summons the Cragmites back into the universe.

His plan has succeeded.

The Cragmites return, and Tachyon immediately launches an invasion across the Polaris Galaxy.

During the chaos, Ratchet and Clank are separated.

Ratchet is knocked unconscious and falls into underground caves filled with Cragmites.

Clank, meanwhile, is helped by the Zoni.

They show him another vision, apparently revealing that Ratchet is in danger.

Using the powers given to him by the Zoni, Clank manages to escape and reunite with Ratchet.

But there is no time to celebrate.

Tachyon's newly restored Cragmite army launches an assault on Meridian City, the capital of the Free Polaris worlds.

Ratchet and Clank rush there to defend the city.

They manage to repel the invasion and rescue Qwark, who has once again managed to find himself in the middle of the disaster.

After the battle, Clank reveals what the Zoni have shown him.

He believes Tachyon is heading back to Fastoon.

Ratchet finally accepts Clank's visions and understands that Fastoon is where the final confrontation will take place.

The group returns to the Lombax homeworld.

They fight their way through Tachyon's army and eventually reach the Court of Azimuth, the heart of the ancient Lombax civilization.

There, Tachyon finally reveals the truth about Ratchet's past.

When Tachyon invaded Fastoon years earlier, the Lombaxes knew they could not defeat him.

Their only chance was to use the Dimensionator to escape into another dimension.

Almost the entire Lombax population left the universe.

But two Lombaxes remained behind.

One of them was the guardian of the Dimensionator.

The other was his infant son.

Ratchet.

Ratchet finally understands the truth.

His father was the Lombax who stayed behind to protect the Dimensionator.

He tried to save his son by sending him away to the distant planet Veldin, in the Solana Galaxy.

Tachyon killed Ratchet's father, but Ratchet survived.

He had unknowingly spent his entire life believing that he was the last Lombax in existence.

Now he knows that this is not true.

His people are alive.

They are simply living in another dimension.

Tachyon then opens a portal to the Lombax dimension.

He offers Ratchet a choice.

He can step through the portal and finally join his own people.

Or he can remain behind and continue fighting Tachyon.

For Ratchet, this should be the moment he has dreamed of his entire life.

He finally has the chance to go home.

But Ratchet realizes something even more important.

As long as Tachyon possesses the Dimensionator, no one is safe.

Tachyon could follow the Lombaxes into their dimension, bring the Cragmites there, or use the device to conquer countless other worlds.

Ratchet therefore refuses Tachyon's offer.

He chooses Clank, Talwyn and his friends, and decides that the Dimensionator must be destroyed.

The final battle begins.

Ratchet and Clank fight Tachyon while Talwyn, Cronk, and Zephyr provide support.

As the battle progresses, the Dimensionator begins malfunctioning.

The device opens an unstable portal, pulling Ratchet and Tachyon into an unknown dimension.

Clank follows.

The three find themselves on a massive asteroid surrounded by the collapsing fabric of the dimension.

Ratchet eventually defeats Tachyon.

Even as he loses, Tachyon insists that Ratchet cannot escape his destiny.

He claims that only he knows Ratchet's true purpose and the truth about his father.

Tachyon is then swallowed by the unstable portal and disappears into another dimension.

But Ratchet and Clank are trapped.

The Dimensionator has been damaged during the battle and can no longer function correctly.

Ratchet discovers that a component of the device is missing.

Fortunately, Clank remembers the strange object given to them earlier by the Plumber on Sargasso: a three-and-three-quarter centicubit hexagonal washer.

It turns out to be exactly what they need.

Clank repairs the Dimensionator, allowing Ratchet to activate it one final time.

Ratchet gives the command:

"Dimensionator, find home."

The device opens a portal and brings Ratchet and Clank back to their friends.

For the first time, Ratchet knows the truth about his origins.

He knows where his people are.

He knows that he has a home.

But he has chosen to remain with Clank and the friends he has made in this universe.

Back aboard the Apogee Space Station, everyone celebrates their victory.

Qwark attempts to learn the pirate code from Rusty Pete, while Cronk and Zephyr humorously reenact Ratchet's battle with Tachyon.

Ratchet, however, remains troubled by one question.

Tachyon claimed that Ratchet had a greater purpose.

Ratchet wonders whether the Emperor was telling the truth.

Clank reassures him.

He tells Ratchet that Tachyon may have been right about one thing: Ratchet had a purpose.

And he has already fulfilled it by defeating Tachyon and saving the galaxy.

For a moment, everything seems peaceful again.

Then something unexpected happens.

The Zoni appear, and this time, they are visible to everyone.

They surround Clank and trap him inside a strange energy field.

Ratchet immediately tries to stop them.

The Zoni explain that Clank is ready to learn the truth about his own existence and his true purpose.

Despite Ratchet's protests, they open a dimensional portal and take Clank away.

Ratchet is left completely helpless as his best friend disappears.

The game ends with Ratchet staring at the place where Clank was taken.

The Lombax mystery has finally been solved.

But a new mystery has just begun:

Who are the Zoni, what is Clank's true purpose, and where have they taken him?

This leads directly into the next chapter of the Future saga, Ratchet & Clank Future: Quest for Booty.

Modes

  • Story (Solo) — Emperor Percival Tachyon hunts the last Lombax across the Polaris galaxy, while Clank meets the mysterious Zoni.
  • Challenge Mode (New Game+) — Replay with your arsenal, with a bolt multiplier and more weapon levels.
  • Imperial Fight Festival (Arena) — Arena challenges with bolt prizes.
  • Space combat (Variations) — Ship sequences between planets.

Economy

  • Bolts — Main currency, dropped by enemies and crates; spent at Gadgetron vendors on weapons, gadgets and ammo.
  • Raritanium — Spent on weapon upgrade grids.
  • Gold Bolts — Hidden collectibles that unlock skins and cheats.
  • Holo-plans — Collect them to build special weapons such as the RYNO IV.
  • Zoni — Found by Clank; they upgrade his abilities and the ship.

Progression

  • Weapons level up with use; Raritanium unlocks extra upgrade nodes.
  • Skill Points return as optional challenges.
