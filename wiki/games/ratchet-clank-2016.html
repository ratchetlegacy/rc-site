Story

## Ratchet & Clank (2016)

The story begins in Aleero City, where the former Captain of the Galactic Rangers, Captain Qwark, has been imprisoned.

Qwark is sharing a cell with a prisoner named Shiv Helix, who happens to be a huge fan of the famous hero.

When Shiv mentions that a new "hologame" is being created about Qwark's latest adventure, Qwark immediately agrees to tell him the story.

However, this is Qwark's version of events—and as the story progresses, it becomes clear that his narration is not always completely reliable.

### The Beginning

Several months earlier, the Solana Galaxy was thrown into panic after several planets mysteriously disappeared.

On the planet Veldin, a young Lombax named Ratchet dreams of becoming a Galactic Ranger.

Ratchet works as a spaceship mechanic under the guidance of his mentor Grimroth Razz, but he is desperate to leave Veldin and become a hero.

When the Galactic Rangers arrive on Veldin to recruit new members, Ratchet immediately takes part in the selection process.

Although he performs well during the physical tests, Captain Qwark discovers Ratchet's criminal record.

Because of his previous offenses, Qwark rejects him.

Ratchet returns home disappointed.

At the same time, something is happening on the planet Quartu.

Chairman Alonzo Drek, leader of the Blarg, is overseeing the construction of a gigantic robot army.

The army is being developed with the help of the brilliant but dangerous scientist Dr. Nefarious, a former Galactic Ranger who has become one of the Rangers' greatest enemies.

During the production of the warbots, a small defective robot is created.

The robot possesses information about Drek's plans and realizes that he must escape before he is destroyed.

He steals a small spacecraft and attempts to flee the factory.

However, Drek's lieutenant, Victor Von Ion, pursues him and damages the ship.

The damaged craft eventually crashes on Veldin.

Ratchet discovers the wreckage and rescues the tiny robot before the ship explodes.

The robot explains that he must warn the Galactic Rangers about Drek.

Ratchet decides to help him.

He gives the robot a name:

Clank.

The two immediately set off for the Rangers' headquarters on Kerwan.

### The Attack on Kerwan

Before reaching Kerwan, Ratchet and Clank are forced to land on Novalis after their ship is attacked.

They help the inhabitants and eventually continue their journey.

When they finally arrive at Kerwan, the planet is already under attack.

Drek's army has launched a massive assault against the Galactic Rangers.

Ratchet and Clank join the battle.

Using Ratchet's ship and its magnetic system, they manage to destroy large numbers of warbots and help repel the invasion.

Their actions impress the Rangers—and the public.

Although Qwark is reluctant to give Ratchet any credit, the Rangers eventually allow Ratchet and Clank to join the team.

For Ratchet, his dream has finally come true.

### The Galactic Rangers

Ratchet begins training alongside Captain Qwark, Cora Veralux, and Brax Lectrus, while Elaris provides technical support.

Ratchet quickly proves that he is an extremely capable fighter.

Clank, meanwhile, helps Elaris analyze the information he recovered from Drek's forces.

The Rangers discover that Drek is using a gigantic weapon called the Deplanetizer.

The weapon is capable of completely destroying planets.

Drek's plan is to destroy worlds and use their remains to construct a new planet for the Blarg.

Their homeworld, Quartu, has become severely polluted and is no longer suitable for them to live on.

Drek therefore intends to create a new, perfect world for his people.

The Rangers begin a campaign against Drek Industries.

They travel across the galaxy, destroying warbot factories, gathering information and sabotaging Drek's operations.

Ratchet's popularity continues to grow.

Unfortunately, this creates a problem.

### Qwark's Jealousy

Captain Qwark has always been the galaxy's most famous hero.

But Ratchet's success begins to attract more attention than Qwark's.

Qwark becomes increasingly jealous.

Drek notices this and decides to exploit it.

He secretly approaches Qwark and offers him a deal.

Qwark will help Drek sabotage the Rangers.

In return, Qwark will regain the fame and admiration he believes Ratchet has stolen from him.

Qwark eventually accepts.

He does not initially intend for the Rangers to be killed.

He simply wants Ratchet to fail.

But his decision has catastrophic consequences.

### The Deplanetizer

The Rangers eventually launch an assault against the Deplanetizer.

Their objective is to prevent Drek from destroying another planet.

However, their weapons suddenly malfunction.

The Rangers have unknowingly been betrayed by Qwark.

Ratchet manages to infiltrate the Deplanetizer alone.

Inside, he confronts Drek.

Drek reveals that Qwark has been secretly helping him.

Ratchet is devastated.

Drek then ejects Ratchet into space.

Ratchet watches helplessly as the Deplanetizer fires.

The target is Novalis.

The planet is destroyed.

Although the population has been evacuated beforehand, Ratchet feels responsible.

He believes that he has failed as a hero.

The Rangers are devastated by Qwark's betrayal.

Ratchet returns to Veldin and decides to give up.

### The Final Plan

Clank, however, refuses to let Ratchet abandon the fight.

He discovers that Drek's next target is Umbris.

Unlike the previous planets, Umbris possesses an extremely unstable core.

If Umbris is destroyed at the wrong moment, the resulting chain reaction could destroy countless other worlds.

Ratchet realizes that something is wrong.

Drek may not simply be trying to create a new planet.

Someone else may be manipulating him.

The answer is Dr. Nefarious.

Nefarious has his own grudge against the Galactic Rangers and has been using Drek's ambitions for his own purposes.

His ultimate goal is to destroy the entire galaxy.

Meanwhile, Qwark finally realizes that Drek never intended to honor their agreement.

Drek has already betrayed him.

Nefarious then turns against Drek as well.

Using a weapon, he transforms Drek into a sheep and ejects him into space.

Nefarious takes control of the Deplanetizer.

### Ratchet Returns

Ratchet finally decides to return to the Rangers.

The team travels to Kalebo III, where Ratchet obtains a Holo-Guise, allowing him to disguise himself as Qwark.

Using the device, Ratchet infiltrates the Deplanetizer.

His objective is to remove the weapon's gravity stabilizer.

If the stabilizer is removed, the Rangers will be able to use powerful magnets to move the Deplanetizer away from Umbris.

Ratchet succeeds.

However, Qwark confronts him.

The two former allies fight.

Qwark initially refuses to admit his mistakes, but Ratchet manages to make him understand the consequences of his actions.

Qwark finally realizes that his jealousy nearly destroyed everything he had fought to protect.

He admits his mistake and accepts Ratchet as the better hero.

He even names Ratchet the new commander of the Galactic Rangers.

### Nefarious' Final Attack

Before the Rangers can capture Nefarious, the mad scientist activates the Deplanetizer.

The weapon begins charging toward Umbris.

The Rangers activate their magnetic system and manage to pull the Deplanetizer away from its target.

Nefarious is furious.

He escapes in his enormous personal battle mech and heads toward a nearby dwarf star.

His new plan is even more extreme.

He intends to trigger a massive stellar explosion that will destroy the entire galaxy.

Ratchet and Clank pursue him.

They engage Nefarious in battle and eventually destroy his mech.

Nefarious is thrown directly into the star.

He appears to be destroyed.

At the same time, the Deplanetizer begins falling toward Umbris.

Ratchet, Clank and Qwark manage to escape using a teleporter just before the weapon is destroyed.

The galaxy has been saved.

### Back to the Present

The story returns to the prison.

Qwark finishes telling Shiv Helix his version of events.

Later, Qwark is working around the Hall of Heroes, where he sees a statue of himself.

Ratchet and Clank arrive.

Qwark is happy to see them.

However, Shiv Helix suddenly escapes.

He steals a weapon, destroys several patrol robots and hijacks Ratchet's ship.

Ratchet and Clank prepare to chase him.

Ratchet then turns to Qwark and asks if he wants to come with them.

Qwark happily accepts.

The adventure ends with Ratchet, Clank and Qwark once again heading into space together.

Did you know?

Because Qwark narrates, fans treat the 2002 original as the "true" canon version.

Modes

  • Story (Solo) — Reimagining of the first game alongside the film: Ratchet joins the Galactic Rangers to fight Chairman Drek and Dr. Nefarious.
  • Challenge Mode (New Game+) — Replay with your arsenal; weapons reach Omega versions and a bolt multiplier rewards streaks.
  • Hoverboard races (Mini-game) — Hoverboard races with prizes.
    Maps: Rilgar
  • Clank sections (Variations) — Puzzle sections with Gadgebots.

Economy

  • Bolts — Main currency, dropped by enemies and crates; spent at Gadgetron vendors on weapons, gadgets and ammo.
  • Raritanium — Spent on weapon upgrade grids.
  • Gold Bolts — Hidden collectibles that unlock cheats and extras.
  • Holocards — Collectible cards; complete sets give bonuses, and the RYNO cards build the RYNO VII.
