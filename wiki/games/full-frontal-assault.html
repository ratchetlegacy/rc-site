🎮 Full Frontal Assault

PlayStation 3 · 2012

PlatformPlayStation 3
Year2012
PlayableRatchet, Clank, Captain Qwark
Release27 November 2012 (NA) · 28 November 2012 (EU)
DeveloperInsomniac Games

A compact entry blending classic action with tower-defense strategy — defend your base while capturing enemy territory.

Story

The story begins with Ratchet, Clank, and Captain Qwark enjoying some time together after their latest adventures.

Their peaceful moment is interrupted when they learn that an old enemy has returned.

The villain behind the new threat is Vendra, who has managed to obtain a powerful weapon known as the Deplanetizer.

Vendra's plan is to use the weapon to attack the galaxy and force its inhabitants to surrender.

Ratchet, Clank, and Qwark immediately set out to stop her.

The three heroes travel to several planets threatened by Vendra's forces.

However, they quickly discover that simply attacking the enemy is not enough.

Vendra's army is constructing powerful bases across the planets, protected by defensive structures and waves of enemies.

Ratchet, Clank, and Qwark must therefore establish their own bases and defend them while simultaneously destroying the enemy's installations.

As they progress through the galaxy, the heroes discover that Vendra is not working alone.

She has assembled a powerful army and is using advanced technology to strengthen her forces.

Ratchet and Clank begin investigating the source of her power.

Their investigation eventually leads them to an ancient technology capable of controlling enormous amounts of energy.

Vendra intends to use this technology to amplify the power of the Deplanetizer and turn it into a weapon capable of destroying entire worlds.

The heroes launch a series of attacks against Vendra's bases.

Each battle requires them to build defensive towers, protect their own base, and destroy the enemy's generators.

Qwark, as usual, considers himself the greatest strategist in the galaxy and constantly takes credit for the team's successes.

Ratchet is much less impressed by Qwark's attitude, while Clank tries to keep the two working together.

Despite their differences, the three heroes gradually make progress.

They destroy several of Vendra's major installations and weaken her army.

Eventually, they discover the location of Vendra's main base.

The heroes launch their final assault.

After fighting through waves of enemies and destroying the defensive network protecting the base, Ratchet, Clank, and Qwark reach Vendra's weapon.

Vendra activates the Deplanetizer and attempts to use it against the galaxy.

Ratchet and Clank manage to sabotage the weapon before it can reach full power.

The resulting overload destroys Vendra's machinery and defeats her forces.

The galaxy is saved once again.

Ratchet, Clank, and Qwark return victorious.

Although the adventure has once again placed the galaxy in danger, the three heroes have proven that teamwork and a good defense can overcome even the most heavily armed enemy.

Their latest victory marks another chapter in their long history of saving the galaxy.

Did you know?

One of the shortest, most budget-friendly releases, aimed at digital players.

Modes

  • Campaign (Solo / co-op) — Mix of action and tower defense: defend your base generators and destroy the enemy's.
  • Competitive (Multiplayer) — Online one-against-one base battles.

Economy

  • Bolts — Main currency, dropped by enemies and crates; spent at Gadgetron vendors on weapons, gadgets and ammo.
  • Bolts (defenses) — Bolts are also spent on turrets, barriers and mines to protect your base.
