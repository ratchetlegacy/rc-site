🎮 Size Matters

PlayableRatchet, Clank

ProgressionWeapons level up with use., Skill Points return as optional challenges.

Ratchet and Clank investigate the mystery of the Technomites, a secretive race of tiny inventors, across a surprisingly meaty handheld campaign.

Story

It all began when Ratchet and Clank took a well-deserved vacation at Jowai Resort on Pokitaru, when a young girl approached them.

Her name was Luna, and she interrupted Ratchet while she was working on a school project about heroes.

She asked Ratchet if she could take his photograph, provided that he was willing to fight some robots for her.

Touched by the young girl's request and being far too kind for his own good, Ratchet decided to help her.

However, just as Ratchet was finishing his little exercises, the station was attacked by mysterious robots, and the young girl was kidnapped, leaving behind an unknown artifact.

Without hesitation, the duo set off in pursuit of the kidnappers, who appeared to be searching for the artifact.

### On the trail of the Technomites

The artifact dropped by the kidnappers points the duo to the jungle planet Ryllus. There, they discover that Captain Qwark has stowed away aboard their ship: he is searching for his long-lost family.

Inside the largest building on Ryllus, Ratchet and Clank find a Technomite map room, which gives them coordinates for Kalidon. Meanwhile, Qwark follows leads from a family-tree website called Faux-Family.com, unaware that he is being watched.

On Kalidon, Ratchet wins a skyboard race and earns the Shrink Ray. Inside a local factory, the duo defeats a ferocious beast named Mungo and finally catches up with Luna.

### Betrayal

Luna reveals that the kidnapping was an act. Ratchet is knocked out and sent to Medical Outpost Omega to be operated on, while Clank is dumped on Metalis, right in the middle of Robot War III.

Clank escapes and tracks Ratchet down thanks to Skrunch, who has obtained footage of the Lombax's surgery. While unconscious, Ratchet wanders through Dreamtime, a strange dream full of characters and enemies from his past, until Clank wakes him up.

The duo destroys the space station and learns that Luna is working with the Technomites. On Challax, they discover Technomite City and the truth: Luna is a robot.

### The clone army

On Dayni Moon, Luna reveals the Technomites' goal: they used Ratchet's DNA to build an army of Ratchet clones, tired of never getting credit for the technology they invented.

After Luna's defeat, Technomites invade Clank's body and shut him down. Ratchet shrinks himself to go inside his friend and repair him.

The duo then reaches the Clone Factory on Quodrona, where Ratchet fights his way through the clone army to the Technomite emperor, Otto Destruct. Otto plans to use his clones and a new machine to steal the brain power of every intelligent being and become the smartest being in the universe.

During the battle, Qwark gets in the way, convinced that Otto is his father. After Otto's defeat, Skrunch brings proof of who Qwark's real parents were, and that they are no longer alive.

Furious, Qwark tries to use Otto's machine on himself, but Otto shrinks him. Ratchet then triggers the machine by accident, swapping Otto's intelligence with Skrunch's.

Back in Clank's apartment in Megapolis, the duo relaxes in front of a commercial for "Battle Ratchets", the clones now sold as toys, while a tiny Qwark brags about his exploits to a group of miniature Ratchets.

Did you know?

It was later ported to PS2 — proof the handheld entries were more than just spin-offs.

Modes

  • Story (Solo · PSP) — On holiday, Ratchet and Clank chase the kidnappers of a young girl, Luna, and uncover the Technomites.
  • Skyboard races (Mini-game) — Skyboarding races with prizes.
  • Clank sections (Variations) — Clank-only levels, including giant Clank fights.
  • Multiplayer (Multiplayer) — Ad hoc battles for several PSP players.

Economy

  • Bolts — Main currency, dropped by enemies and crates; spent at Gadgetron vendors on weapons, gadgets and ammo.
  • Titanium Bolts — Hidden collectibles that unlock extras.
  • Armor pieces — Collected throughout the game; full sets grant special effects.
