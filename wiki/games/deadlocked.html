🎮 Deadlocked

PlayStation 2 · 2005

PlatformPlayStation 2
Year2005
TeamRatchet, Merc, Green
Release25 October 2005 (NA) · 18 November 2005 (EU)
DeveloperInsomniac Games

Ratchet is kidnapped and forced to compete in DreadZone, a deadly televised gladiator tournament. The most combat-focused classic entry.

Story

Ratchet, Clank, and Al are captured aboard the Phoenix by recruiters from DreadZone, a holovision program where contestants, most often forcibly recruited from among renowned heroes known for their combat abilities, are forced to fight each other in deadly matches.

The creator of the program is Gleeman Vox, the owner of the pay-per-view channel Pox. Vox is motivated solely by money, and the purpose of DreadZone is to boost his channel's ratings and make a fortune by selling a wide range of merchandise.

With this in mind, Vox has assembled DreadZone's best fighters into a team known as the Exterminators, consisting of Shellshock, Reactor, The Eviscerator, and Ace Hardlight, a former hero and five-time DreadZone champion.

Vox wants to turn the Exterminators into beloved celebrities so he can sell merchandise bearing their likenesses.

However, sales of Ace Hardlight merchandise fail to take off because the audience dislikes his arrogant personality and cruelty, which infuriates Vox.

Ratchet is therefore forced to fight robots created by Pox Industries, another company owned by Vox, as well as other contestants.

Clank is assigned the role of assistant and must help his Lombax friend from a computer in the control room of the DreadZone Station, which houses the gladiators' cells and the Combat Dome, where the most important matches take place.

Al, meanwhile, is tasked with maintaining and upgrading two combat robots, Merc and Green, who assist Ratchet during his missions.

In addition, Ratchet and his friends, like all the gladiators, are fitted with a collar capable of electrocuting its wearer and making them explode if they rebel, attempt to escape, or become boring to watch.

Ratchet wins the qualification match and officially enters the competition.

At first, he appears to be seduced by the gladiator lifestyle, while Clank is much more aware of their true status as prisoners.

Because of Ratchet's small size, Vox underestimates him and believes he cannot possibly stand a chance against the Exterminators.

Regular reports are therefore broadcast on Pox News, in which Ratchet is accused of being a mafioso, a vandal, an illegal Hoverbike dealer, and many other things.

Vox's goal is to create a negative image of Ratchet in order to increase ratings and profits when he is eventually killed by an Exterminator.

However, nothing goes according to plan.

Ratchet survives every campaign and eliminates two of the four Exterminators, while his popularity among the fans continues to grow.

Clank tells Ratchet that he believes he has found a way to free all the gladiators being held against their will.

When Ratchet fights The Eviscerator, Clank takes advantage of the security screens being disabled to continue his investigation.

After defeating the third Exterminator, Ratchet reunites with Clank and Al.

Al has just managed to deactivate and remove Clank's collar.

Suddenly, Ace Hardlight appears and shoots Al on Vox's orders before forcing Clank to put his collar back on.

Ratchet is furious with Ace and with himself, because he had enjoyed his life as a gladiator and had not truly been trying to escape.

He considers himself responsible for what happened to Al.

Ratchet then faces Ace Hardlight in the Combat Dome and wins the match.

Ace, defeated for the first time, realizes what he has become and warns Ratchet, the new number-one fighter in DreadZone, not to let Vox corrupt him as he himself had been corrupted by fame and money.

Ace eventually collapses, and Pox News announces his death, although he has actually survived the encounter.

Ratchet is then summoned by Vox, who wants to make him the new captain of the Exterminators.

Vox has realized how popular Ratchet has become and believes that merchandise bearing Ratchet's image will be a huge commercial success.

However, Ratchet refuses the offer.

Rather than simply detonating Ratchet's collar, which could disappoint the fans and cause ratings to drop if Ratchet were not killed during a match, Vox sends him on one final campaign in the Ghost Station, an unexplored area of DreadZone that has never been tested and from which survival is considered impossible.

However, with Clank's help, Ratchet manages to emerge victorious from the challenge and returns to DreadZone Station.

There, he discovers that Al is now half-cyborg.

Al explains that he and Clank have located the computer controlling the containment fields holding all the imprisoned heroes.

They have also hacked a shuttle so that it will take Ratchet to the level of the station where the computer is located.

During the journey, Ratchet learns that, following the shuttle hack, all transportation throughout the station has been suspended, preventing him from leaving the station.

When he arrives at the computer, Ratchet receives a message from Vox announcing that he has discovered their plan.

To add tension and boost the ratings, Vox has connected the station to six gigatons of nitroglycerin.

Ratchet now has a limited amount of time to disarm the explosives and release the escape shuttles before the station explodes, killing the heroes, the audience, and Vox himself.

Ratchet manages to deactivate the generators, and the station is evacuated.

Vox then arrives aboard the station piloting a combat robot and challenges Ratchet.

Ratchet wins the duel, but Vox activates the station's self-destruct sequence, believing that he will achieve record-breaking ratings if Ratchet and he die at the same time.

Clank then appears at the controls of an escape shuttle and rescues Ratchet.

Vox, trapped beneath the wreckage of his combat robot, remains aboard the station as it explodes, marking the end of DreadZone.

Aboard the shuttle, Ratchet and Clank receive messages of thanks from heroes who were able to escape thanks to them, including Kid Nova and Aquagirl.

They also receive a message from Captain Qwark, who tells them about a rumor he has heard concerning an organization that kidnaps galactic heroes and forces them to fight to the death.

Qwark insists that the rumor cannot possibly be true, since nobody has ever tried to kidnap him.

After the credits, a cutscene shows Doctor Nefarious and his butler Lawrence, still stranded on an asteroid since the events of Ratchet & Clank 3.

They arrive at the location of DreadZone Station and discover that it has exploded, eliminating any chance of them being rescued anytime soon.

Did you know?

Clank sits most of this one out — he is Ratchet's mission-control voice from the sidelines.

Modes

  • DreadZone story (Solo) — Ratchet is forced to fight in Gleeman Vox's DreadZone, a deadly TV show, alongside his combat bots Merc and Green.
  • Co-op campaign (2 players) — The whole campaign can be played with a second player in split screen.
  • Challenge Mode (New Game+) — Replay with your arsenal and push weapons further.
  • Online multiplayer (Multiplayer) — Online and local battles such as Deathmatch, King of the Hill and Capture the Flag.

Economy

  • Bolts — Main currency, dropped by enemies and crates; spent at Gadgetron vendors on weapons, gadgets and ammo.
  • Dread Points — Earned by completing DreadZone challenges; they unlock extras.
  • Weapon mods — Alpha mods improve stats, Omega mods add special effects.

Progression

  • Weapons level up with use and take Alpha and Omega mods.
  • Merc and Green follow your orders and can be upgraded.
