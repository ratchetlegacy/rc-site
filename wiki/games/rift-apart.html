A dimension-hopping showcase for the PS5. Dr. Nefarious tears open rifts between realities, introducing Rivet — a Lombax from another dimension.

Story

## Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart

The story begins in two different dimensions.

In one dimension, a young Lombax named Rivet is fighting against the oppressive Nefarious Empire.

In Rivet's dimension, the Emperor has conquered most of the galaxy and rules through fear.

His capital is Nefarious City, built on the planet Corson V.

Rivet is one of the most important members of the Resistance.

She has spent years fighting against the Emperor and his army, becoming one of his greatest enemies.

During one Resistance mission, Rivet disguises herself as a robot and steals an important infobot containing information about the Emperor.

She manages to escape Nefarious City and brings the information back to the Resistance.

Meanwhile, in Ratchet and Clank's dimension, the two heroes are preparing for the Festival of Heroes.

Ratchet has become one of the galaxy's greatest heroes, while Clank has been working on something extremely important.

Clank has rebuilt the Dimensionator.

His intention is to give Ratchet the possibility of finding the other Lombaxes.

Ratchet has always wondered whether he should search for his species, and Clank believes that his friend deserves the opportunity to finally meet them.

However, their celebration is interrupted by an attack.

Dr. Nefarious has returned.

He steals the Dimensionator and attempts to use it against Ratchet and Clank.

During the battle, Ratchet fires at the Dimensionator and damages its Phase Quartz core.

The device becomes unstable.

Rifts begin appearing everywhere.

The dimensional boundaries start collapsing.

Ratchet and Clank pursue Nefarious through the chaos, but the damaged Dimensionator eventually transports them to another dimension.

This is the dimension ruled by the Emperor Nefarious.

The Dimensionator explodes shortly after their arrival.

Clank is badly damaged and separated from Ratchet.

Dr. Nefarious is also separated from them.

However, something unexpected happens.

The inhabitants of this dimension mistake Dr. Nefarious for their ruler.

Their real Emperor is absent.

Dr. Nefarious immediately realizes that this is an opportunity.

He decides to pretend that he is the Emperor.

And for once in his life, Nefarious has apparently won.

### Ratchet Meets Rivet

Ratchet wakes up in Nefarious City, which is very different from the city he knows.

The entire planet is controlled by the Empire.

Ratchet encounters a Resistance fighter known as Phantom, who helps him escape the Imperial forces.

Phantom explains that the Resistance has been fighting the Emperor for years.

Ratchet eventually reaches Sargasso, where he meets Rivet.

Rivet has found Clank.

However, she does not initially trust him.

Clank explains that he comes from another dimension and that Ratchet is searching for him.

Rivet eventually realizes that Clank is telling the truth.

The two begin working together.

Ratchet, meanwhile, discovers that the Resistance is trying to locate Captain Quantum, a dimensional counterpart of Captain Qwark.

Ratchet and Rivet therefore split up.

Ratchet and Clank search for Captain Quantum.

Rivet and Clank's counterpart begin investigating the Emperor.

### Rivet and Kit

Rivet's new companion is a mysterious robot called KT-7461.

Rivet eventually gives her the name Kit.

Kit is extremely powerful but has serious doubts about herself.

She was originally created by the Emperor as one of his most powerful warbots.

Years earlier, Kit attacked Rivet during one of Rivet's attempts to infiltrate the Emperor's tower.

Kit blasted Rivet's right arm off.

But Kit stopped herself before killing her.

The incident terrified Kit.

She abandoned the Empire and disappeared.

Rivet later built herself a mechanical replacement arm.

Despite her hatred of the Empire, Rivet still has a deep mistrust of robots.

This makes her relationship with Kit complicated from the beginning.

### The Emperor Returns

Ratchet and Rivet eventually reunite at Zurkie's, a Battleplex located in the Scarstu Debris Field.

They finally meet face-to-face.

For Ratchet, it is the first time he has ever met another Lombax.

For Rivet, meeting Ratchet is equally extraordinary.

They discover that the two of them have much more in common than they expected.

However, their reunion is interrupted.

The real Emperor Nefarious returns.

Unlike Dr. Nefarious, the Emperor has never experienced failure.

He has conquered his dimension and believes that he is destined to rule everything.

He takes control of the Dimensionator.

The Emperor decides that conquering a single dimension is no longer enough.

He wants to conquer every dimension.

To accomplish this, he needs the Dimensional Map, located in the Interdimensional Archives on Savali.

### The Assault on Sargasso

The Emperor launches an invasion of Sargasso.

Rivet and Kit return to defend their home.

The Emperor's forces deploy enormous warships and thousands of troops.

The planet is almost destroyed.

Rivet and Kit fight their way through the invasion while helping the Morts and other Resistance fighters.

They also use Trudi, a friendly flying creature capable of launching powerful fireballs.

The Emperor eventually orders his ships to activate their Destruct-o Beams.

Their objective is to destabilize Sargasso's core and destroy the entire planet.

Rivet and Kit manage to destroy the battle cruisers before they can complete the operation.

Sargasso survives.

But the Emperor is not finished.

He uses the Dimensionator to trap Rivet and Kit inside a pocket dimension.

They manage to escape.

During their journey, Kit finally reveals part of her past.

Rivet realizes that Kit was the warbot responsible for destroying her arm years earlier.

Kit is horrified.

Rivet is devastated.

Their relationship is suddenly placed in danger.

### The Dimensional Map

The heroes learn that the Emperor has already attacked the Interdimensional Archives on Savali.

His goal is to obtain the Dimensional Map.

The Emperor needs the map to locate and invade other dimensions.

Ratchet and Clank travel to Savali while Rivet and Kit infiltrate the Emperor's flagship.

They rescue Gary, a cosmic prophet who knows how to access the dimensional anomalies.

Gary explains that the Monks hid the Dimensional Map inside a dimensional anomaly so the Emperor could not find it.

Ratchet and Clank enter the catacombs beneath Savali.

Clank enters the anomaly and manages to retrieve the knowledge necessary to stop the dimensional catastrophe.

But the two Nefariouses ambush them.

The Emperor and Dr. Nefarious steal the map.

Ratchet and Clank are captured and sent to Zordoom Prison.

### Kit's Secret

Rivet and Kit arrive too late.

Kit decides that she can no longer hide from her past.

She transforms into her original warbot form and attacks the Emperor.

Rivet watches in horror as she realizes the truth.

Kit was the machine that took her arm.

Kit fires at the Emperor, but Dr. Nefarious intervenes.

He uses the Dimensionator to send Kit into a dimensional rift.

Rivet is left behind.

She believes she has lost Kit.

Later, however, Kit returns.

She has finally decided to stop running from what she was created to be.

Rivet accepts her back.

The two become partners again.

### The Escape from Zordoom

Rivet, Ratchet, Clank, Kit and their allies launch an assault on Zordoom Prison.

They free the prisoners and rescue their friends.

With their forces united, they prepare for one final confrontation with Emperor Nefarious.

The Emperor broadcasts his plan across the galaxy.

He intends to conquer every dimension.

His enormous fleet gathers around Corson V.

He opens a massive dimensional rift leading directly into Ratchet's dimension.

The invasion begins.

### The Final Battle

Ratchet and Clank arrive in Megalopolis, now under attack by the Emperor's army.

The city is being torn apart by dimensional rifts.

Buildings and structures from different dimensions appear throughout the battlefield.

The Resistance, pirates, Monks and other allies join the battle.

Rivet and Kit attack the Emperor's forces.

Meanwhile, Dr. Nefarious begins to realize something.

The Emperor is becoming increasingly obsessed with his own success.

The Doctor has spent his entire life trying to prove that he is the greatest villain in the universe.

But the Emperor simply has everything he ever wanted.

The Emperor even begins insulting and humiliating Dr. Nefarious.

Eventually, the Doctor snaps.

He decides that he will no longer accept being treated as a subordinate.

### The Imperial Power Suit

The Emperor enters his gigantic Imperial Power Suit.

The enormous machine attacks the Resistance.

Ratchet, Clank, Rivet, Kit and their allies fight back.

The machine is almost unstoppable.

Ratchet eventually enters the Power Suit's heart chamber.

With the help of the Savali Monks, he reaches its power source.

Ratchet destroys it.

The gigantic machine collapses.

The Emperor survives.

But he is furious.

He retrieves the Dimensionator and begins using it recklessly.

Thousands of dimensional rifts open across the battlefield.

The dimensional barriers are collapsing.

If the Emperor continues, every dimension could be destroyed.

### Rivet and Kit vs. the Emperor

The Emperor turns his attention toward Rivet.

He mocks her and insists that she should surrender.

Rivet refuses.

Kit joins her.

For the first time, Kit openly rejects the Emperor.

She tells him that she is no longer his weapon.

The two fight together.

They defeat the Emperor's remaining troops.

The Emperor begins to lose control.

Desperate to win, he overcharges the Dimensionator.

The device starts collapsing the dimensions themselves.

Kit launches Rivet toward the Emperor.

Rivet strikes him with her hammer.

The Emperor falls.

Ratchet, Clank and their allies recover the Dimensionator.

The Emperor desperately reaches for help.

He asks Dr. Nefarious to save him.

But the Doctor has finally had enough.

Instead of helping him, Dr. Nefarious mocks the Emperor.

He reminds him that all of his supposed success has ultimately led to failure.

Dr. Nefarious kicks the Emperor's hand away.

The Emperor falls into a dimensional rift.

His reign finally comes to an end.

### Restoring the Dimensions

The Dimensionator is still unstable.

Clank realizes that the damage can be reversed.

Using the Dimensionator, he closes the dimensional rifts and restores the boundaries between the universes.

The dimensional catastrophe is finally stopped.

Rivet and Kit reconcile completely.

Kit decides that she no longer wants to run from her past.

She wants to remain with Rivet.

Rivet happily accepts her.

Ratchet and Clank also remain together.

For Ratchet, the adventure has changed everything.

He has finally met another Lombax.

And for the first time, he has someone who truly understands what it means to be the last of their kind.

### The Final Journey

After the battle, the heroes begin rebuilding what was destroyed.

The Dimensional Map is returned to the Archives.

Gary and the Monks repair the damage to Savali.

Blizar Prime is slowly being restored.

The Resistance begins rebuilding its worlds.

Ratchet and Clank return to Zurkie's, where everyone celebrates their victory.

Rivet and Kit are now officially partners.

Ratchet introduces Rivet and Kit to some of his closest friends.

And then Ratchet makes one final decision.

He still has the Dimensionator.

He still has the possibility of travelling between dimensions.

This time, however, he will not be travelling alone.

Ratchet, Clank, Rivet and Kit prepare to enter another dimensional rift together.

The four heroes jump through.

Where they are going is unknown.

But one thing is certain:

Ratchet is no longer alone.

He has found another Lombax.

Clank has found another counterpart.

Rivet has found someone who understands her.

And Kit has finally found a place where she belongs.

Their next adventure is only beginning.

Did you know?

The rifts use the PS5's SSD to yank you between whole worlds in a blink.

Modes

  • Story (Solo · PS5) — Dr. Nefarious breaks the Dimensionator: Ratchet and Clank are split up across dimensions and meet Rivet, a Lombax from another reality.
  • Challenge Mode (New Game+) — Replay with your arsenal; weapons go up to level 10 and gain Omega versions.
  • Zurkie's Battleplex (Arena) — Arena challenges run by Zurkie, with bolt and item prizes.
  • Pocket dimensions (Exploration) — Short hidden rifts holding armor pieces.
  • Clank & Glitch sections (Variations) — Clank solves dimension puzzles, Glitch fights viruses inside computers.

Economy

  • Bolts — Main currency, dropped by enemies and crates; spent at Gadgetron vendors on weapons, gadgets and ammo.
  • Raritanium — Spent on weapon upgrade grids.
  • Gold Bolts — Hidden collectibles that unlock cheats and extras.
  • Spybots — Hidden in each world; finding them all unlocks the RYNO VIII.
  • Armor pieces — Found in pocket dimensions; sets give defensive bonuses.
