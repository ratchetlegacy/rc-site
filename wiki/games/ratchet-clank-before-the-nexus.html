🎮 Ratchet & Clank: Before the Nexus

iOS / Android · 2013

PlatformiOS / Android
Year2013

An endless runner on grindrails released as the companion to Into the Nexus. Ratchet and Clank chase the Prog twins across Axiom City after the kidnapping of a Terachnoid scientist, collecting bolts and Raritanium to upgrade their arsenal.

Story

The story is told in the game's animated intro, presented as a Polaris news flash.

Anchor Stone Stonefield interrupts the programme: Terachnoid scientist Fleebar Snuttleblast has gone missing. Some believe he simply skipped work to attend Captain Qwark's vid-comic convention.

The latest reports are far more worrying. Fleebar is believed to have been kidnapped by the twins Vendra and Neftin Prog, already the main suspects in a series of thefts at Pollyx Industries. The company's CEO and his team of Terachnoid scientists could be next.

To attack Pollyx Industries, the Prog twins have hired the mercenaries of Thugs-4-Less. Ratchet and Clank set off after them on the grindrails of Axiom City, chased by an attack ship and jetpack-wearing Thugs.

Along the way they free the Terachnoids held hostage, collect bolts and Raritanium, and upgrade their arsenal from run to run.

Ratchet eventually confronts and defeats the twins, capturing Vendra. The story takes place shortly before Into the Nexus, which opens with Ratchet and Clank escorting the captured Vendra.

Did you know?

Raritanium earned in Before the Nexus could be transferred to Into the Nexus on PS3 through a PlayStation Network account. Announced for the PS3 game's launch on 12 November 2013, the mobile game finally arrived more than a month later. It is now delisted and, as a 32-bit app, no longer runs on iOS 11 and later.

Modes

  • Endless run (Solo · grindrails) — Ratchet rides grindrails and runs through Axiom City: swipe left or right to change rails, swipe up to jump, tap to shoot. Each run starts with three objectives (Skill Points).

Economy

  • Bolts — Collected on the rails and from enemies; spent at the vendor on weapons, upgrades, gadgets and armor.
  • Raritanium — Rare nuggets collected during runs. With a PlayStation Network account, it could be synced to Into the Nexus on PS3 to upgrade weapons faster (servers now closed).
  • Hero Bolts — Rare bolts used to continue a run after a fall; one is awarded for completing a run's three objectives.

Enemies

  • Thugs-4-Less
  • Breegus Wasps
  • Cerullean Swarmers
  • Security Bots
  • Thermosplitters
  • Protoguards
  • The Prog twins

Gadgets

  • Armor Magnetizer — Attracts bolts from a distance for a limited time.
  • Reflector — A shield that deflects attacks.
  • Bolt Multiplier — Multiplies the bolts collected while active.
  • Jetpack — Fly above the rails at high speed, invincible.
  • Tornado Launcher — A miniature tornado that picks up enemies and bolts.
  • Rift Inducer — Opens a black hole that swallows nearby enemies.
  • Groovitron — Makes enemies dance while you take them out.

Weapons

  • Constructo Pistol (starting weapon) — Tap or hold to fire.
  • Constructo Shotgun (800 bolts) — Tap to fire, or hold to widen the spread and release.
  • Buzz Blades (1,125 bolts) — Saw blades that ricochet between targets; hold to autofire.
  • Predator Launcher (2,000 bolts) — Hold and swipe over up to 4 enemies to lock on, then release to fire homing missiles.
  • R.Y.N.O.m (free all the Terachnoids) — A missile and minigun combination: tap for single rounds, hold for continuous fire.

Controls

  • Swipe left / right: change rail or lane
  • Swipe up: jump
  • Tap: shoot
  • Tap a glowing target: Swingshot
  • Tap and hold in the air: Heli-Pack glide
  • Gadget button: use the gadget you picked up
