🎮 Into the Nexus

PlayStation 3 · 2013

PlatformPlayStation 3
Year2013
PlayableRatchet, Clank
Release12 November 2013 (NA) · 22 November 2013 (EU)
DeveloperInsomniac Games
ProgressionWeapons level up with use; Raritanium unlocks extra upgrade nodes.

A return to classic, story-driven form and the close of the Future saga. Ratchet faces the dangerous Vendra Prog.

Story

The story begins sometime after the events of A Crack in Time.

Ratchet and Clank have continued their lives as galactic heroes, but Ratchet still carries the weight of his past.

Although he now knows that the Lombaxes survived by escaping into another dimension, he has chosen to remain in his own universe with Clank.

One day, the two friends are hired to transport two dangerous criminals, the twin sisters Vendra and Neftin Prog, to a maximum-security prison.

The sisters are extremely dangerous and are known for their mastery of dimensional technology.

Ratchet and Clank successfully capture them and transport them aboard their ship.

However, during the journey, Vendra reveals that she has no intention of remaining imprisoned.

She uses her powers to communicate with a mysterious being from another dimension.

Ratchet and Clank soon discover that Vendra has been planning her escape from the beginning.

The sisters manage to overpower their guards and escape.

During the confrontation, Vendra opens a dimensional portal and summons a terrifying creature known as Giant Darkmoon.

The creature attacks Ratchet and Clank's ship, forcing them to crash on the remote planet Yerek.

Ratchet survives the crash, but Clank is seriously damaged.

Ratchet repairs his friend and begins searching for Vendra and Neftin.

Their trail leads Ratchet to the Wasteland, a dangerous region filled with strange creatures and dimensional anomalies.

There, Ratchet meets Talwyn Apogee, who has been investigating the Prog sisters.

Talwyn explains that Vendra and Neftin are not ordinary criminals.

The twins are searching for a mysterious species known as the Nethers.

The Nethers are beings that exist outside the normal dimensions of the universe.

Vendra believes that the Nethers possess incredible power and that she can use them to gain control over the galaxy.

Ratchet realizes that the sisters' plan could create a catastrophe.

He and Clank therefore join forces with Talwyn to stop them.

Their investigation leads them across the dangerous worlds of the Zarkov Sector.

Along the way, they encounter strange creatures, abandoned settlements, and unstable dimensional rifts.

They also meet Cronk and Zephyr, Talwyn's two old warbot companions.

The two robots help Ratchet and Clank track down the Prog sisters.

Eventually, the heroes discover that Vendra has found a way to communicate with a Nether named Gleeman Vox.

Vendra believes that Vox can give her the power she needs.

However, Ratchet discovers that Vendra's relationship with the Nethers is much more dangerous than she realizes.

Vendra does not simply want to communicate with them.

She wants to bring one of them into the physical universe.

Vendra finally succeeds.

She opens a massive dimensional portal and summons a Nether known as Giant Darkmoon.

The creature is enormous and incredibly powerful.

It begins tearing apart the surrounding area and destabilizing reality itself.

Ratchet and Clank attempt to stop it, but Vendra manages to escape with the creature.

Meanwhile, Neftin begins to realize that his sister's plan has gone too far.

Although he helped Vendra with her schemes, he never intended for her to unleash a creature capable of destroying entire worlds.

Neftin eventually betrays his sister and secretly contacts Ratchet.

He explains that Vendra has become obsessed with the Nethers.

She believes that they can give her the power to reshape reality.

Neftin asks Ratchet to help him stop her.

Ratchet agrees, but he does not trust him completely.

Together, Ratchet, Clank, Talwyn, and Neftin continue tracking Vendra.

Their journey eventually leads them to the planet Yerek, where Vendra has established a base.

The heroes infiltrate the facility and discover that Vendra has captured several Nethers.

She has been experimenting on them in an attempt to understand their dimensional powers.

Ratchet is horrified.

Vendra is effectively treating the Nethers as prisoners and using them as tools.

The heroes free the creatures and destroy the facility.

However, Vendra escapes once again.

She takes control of the largest Nether and prepares to unleash its full power.

During the confrontation, Captain Qwark arrives to help Ratchet and Clank.

As usual, Qwark dramatically announces himself as the galaxy's greatest hero.

Despite his exaggerated claims, he proves useful during the battle.

The heroes eventually discover Vendra's final objective.

She intends to use the power of the Nethers to open a permanent portal between dimensions.

If she succeeds, the barriers separating the dimensions will collapse.

Entire worlds could be destroyed.

Ratchet, Clank, Talwyn, Neftin, and Qwark launch their final assault.

They fight their way through Vendra's forces and reach the dimensional portal.

Vendra is waiting for them.

She has already begun merging the dimensions.

Ratchet confronts her and attempts to convince her to stop.

Vendra refuses.

She believes that the Nethers will make her powerful enough to control everything.

The final battle begins.

Ratchet and Clank fight Vendra while Neftin tries to shut down the dimensional machinery.

The machinery begins to overload.

Vendra loses control of the Nether she summoned.

The creature turns against her.

Ratchet and Clank take advantage of the chaos and defeat Vendra.

However, the dimensional portal remains open.

The energy released by the portal threatens to destroy the entire sector.

Neftin realizes that the only way to stop the catastrophe is to close the portal from the inside.

He enters the dimensional gateway.

Vendra attempts to stop him.

The twins fight, and Neftin finally manages to break Vendra's control over the Nether.

The portal begins collapsing.

Ratchet, Clank, Talwyn, and Qwark escape just before the dimensional rift closes.

Neftin barely survives and is pulled back into the normal universe.

Vendra, however, is trapped on the other side.

The threat finally comes to an end.

The dimensional barriers stabilize, and the Zarkov Sector is saved.

Neftin is arrested for his crimes, but he is given a chance to cooperate with the authorities in exchange for helping repair the damage caused by his sister.

Ratchet and Clank return home with Talwyn.

For Ratchet, the adventure has once again reminded him that the universe is full of dangers that cannot always be solved by simply fighting them.

More importantly, Ratchet realizes that his life as a hero is not about chasing glory.

It is about protecting the people he cares about.

Clank remains by his side, as he always has.

The two friends look toward the future, ready for whatever adventure awaits them next.

The events of Into the Nexus therefore close the Future storyline on a quieter and more personal note.

Ratchet has accepted that he cannot change his past or recover everything he lost.

Instead, he chooses to move forward with the friends and family he has found along the way.

Did you know?

Despite its shorter length, fans praised it as a return to what made the classics great.

Modes

  • Story (Solo) — Ratchet and Clank escort the criminal Vendra Prog, who escapes to free her brother Neftin and open a path to the Nether dimension.
  • Challenge Mode (New Game+) — Replay with your arsenal and level weapons further.
  • Destructapalooza arena (Arena) — Arena challenges with bolt prizes.
  • Clank's Netherverse (Variations) — 2D puzzle sections where Clank plays with gravity.

Economy

  • Bolts — Main currency, dropped by enemies and crates; spent at Gadgetron vendors on weapons, gadgets and ammo.
  • Raritanium — Spent on weapon upgrade grids.
  • Gold Bolts — Hidden collectibles that unlock extras.
  • Holo-plans — Build special weapons such as the RYNO VII.
