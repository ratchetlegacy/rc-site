🎮 Ratchet & Clank: Clone Home

Mobile phones (J2ME) · 2006

PlatformMobile phones (J2ME)
Year2006
PlayableRatchet, Clank
ReleaseCancelled — recovered and published online on 8 October 2025
DeveloperJavaground (long credited to Handheld Games)
PublisherSony Pictures Mobile
Planned release1 September 2006

When Gleeman Vox creates billions of digital Ratchet and Clank clones to infect the Mega-Net, two digital copies contact the real heroes and ask for their help. Ratchet and Clank must infiltrate Vox's digital world, destroy the source of the virus and escape before they are trapped forever.

Story

The story takes place during the events surrounding DreadZone.

The media mogul Gleeman Vox has developed a new plan to take control of the galaxy.

Instead of using armies or conventional weapons, Vox intends to create billions of digital copies of Ratchet and Clank.

These copies are designed to spread through the Mega-Net like a virus.

If the plan succeeds, the enormous number of clones will overload and destabilize the galaxy's digital infrastructure.

However, something unexpected happens.

Inside Vox's personal communication device, known as his PCD, two digital copies of Ratchet and Clank become aware of what is happening.

The digital clones realize that they are being used as part of Vox's plan.

They manage to establish contact with the real Ratchet and Clank.

The two versions of the heroes then decide to work together.

The real Ratchet and Clank will hunt down Vox in the physical world, while their digital counterparts will infiltrate his PCD and destroy the source file responsible for generating the clones.

The digital Ratchet and Clank begin exploring Vox's private files and security systems.

They eventually discover that the source file is protected by a Three Name Lock Code.

To access it, they must recover three passwords hidden inside Vox's private folders:

Vox

Mama_Peaches

Brother_Daryle

The digital clones manage to locate the three names and use them to bypass the security system.

They finally reach the source file.

At the same time, the real Ratchet and Clank arrive at Vox's laboratory.

But Vox has anticipated their attack.

He activates a Bio Matter Converter and uses it to digitize the real Ratchet and Clank.

The two heroes are pulled into Vox's PCD.

Now the real Ratchet and Clank are trapped inside the digital world alongside their own copies.

Despite the setback, the digital clones succeed in reaching the source file.

They delete it.

However, destroying the source file causes the connection between the digital clones and the real world to collapse.

The digital Ratchet and Clank sacrifice themselves to ensure that Vox's virus can never be reconstructed.

The real heroes are now trapped inside the PCD.

Ratchet and Clank realize that destroying the source file is not enough.

Vox could potentially rebuild the virus if the PCD remains intact.

They therefore decide to destroy the CPU controlling the device.

They fight through Vox's digital security systems and eventually reach the CPU.

Clank contacts Captain Qwark, who is outside the digital world.

Qwark manages to open a network port that can provide Ratchet and Clank with an escape route.

Ratchet and Clank then overload the CPU.

As the system collapses, they confront a digital manifestation of Gleeman Vox.

After defeating Vox's digital form, the heroes use Qwark's network port to escape.

They effectively "piggyback" on a data transmission and are transported back into the physical world.

Ratchet and Clank survive.

Vox's clone virus has been destroyed.

And the two heroes are ready to return to the DreadZone tournament.

Did you know?

Clone Home was cancelled in 2006 despite reaching gold master status. Almost twenty years later, the complete game was recovered from an old Sony Ericsson W880i and publicly released in October 2025.

Modes

  • Story (Solo · mobile) — Side-scrolling action game made for mobile phones, follow-up to Going Mobile.

Economy

  • Bolts — Main currency, dropped by enemies and crates; spent at Gadgetron vendors on weapons, gadgets and ammo.

History

  • 16 June 2006 — IGN reveals the sequel to Going Mobile, with more weapons and graphical improvements.
  • 2006 — The game reaches the end of development, then is cancelled for reasons never officially explained.
  • 2019 — The YouTuber The Golden Bolt relaunches the search for the lost game.
  • 2021 — Super Gamer Omega Clank finds a person whose Sony Ericsson W880i still holds the game.
  • October 2025 — After cracking the phone's encryption, Emily and Super Gamer Omega Clank extract a complete, playable build and archive it publicly.
