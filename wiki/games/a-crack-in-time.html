🎮 A Crack in Time

PlayStation 3 · 2009

PlatformPlayStation 3
Year2009
PlayableRatchet, Clank
Release27 October 2009 (NA) · 6 November 2009 (EU)
DeveloperInsomniac Games

The emotional culmination of the Future trilogy. Ratchet searches for Clank while Clank discovers his destiny at the Great Clock, learning to manipulate time itself.

Story

One year has passed since the events of Quest for Booty.

Ratchet is still searching for Clank, who was taken away by the mysterious Zoni at the end of their previous adventure.

With the help of Captain Qwark, Ratchet continues his search across the Polaris Galaxy.

Meanwhile, Clank is being held inside a mysterious facility known as the Great Clock.

The Great Clock is a gigantic structure located near the center of the universe. It was created by the Zoni to maintain the stability of time itself.

The Clock is not meant to be used as a time machine.

Its purpose is to repair temporal anomalies and prevent the universe from being damaged by changes to the timeline.

Clank awakens inside the Clock and meets Sigmund, a Junior Caretaker responsible for maintaining the facility.

Sigmund explains that the Clock was created by a mysterious Zoni known as Orvus.

Clank soon discovers that Orvus is much more important to him than he first realized.

Orvus is not only the creator of the Great Clock.

He is also Clank's father and true creator.

Orvus created Clank with a specific purpose in mind: Clank was destined to become the next Senior Caretaker of the Great Clock.

However, before Clank can fully understand his destiny, he must help Sigmund repair the damaged Clock.

The Great Clock has been severely damaged by Doctor Nefarious.

After taking Clank from the Zoni, Nefarious spent more than a year attempting to access Clank's memories in order to discover how to enter the mysterious Orvus Chamber, the central control room of the Clock.

Nefarious believes that controlling the Orvus Chamber will allow him to manipulate time itself.

When the Zoni refuse to give him access, Nefarious turns against them.

He activates a powerful device known as the Hypersonic Brainwave Scrambler, attacking the Zoni and damaging the Great Clock.

The attack creates temporal anomalies throughout the universe.

It also inadvertently allows Clank to escape from Nefarious's control.

While Clank begins repairing the Great Clock, Ratchet and Qwark continue searching for him.

Their search eventually takes them to the Breegus System, where they encounter the mercenary forces of Nefarious.

Ratchet is attacked by Lord Vorselon, Nefarious's personal assassin.

Vorselon is searching for another Lombax named Alister Azimuth.

Because of their similar appearance, Ratchet is initially mistaken for Azimuth.

After learning about this mysterious Lombax, Ratchet decides to find him.

He eventually discovers Azimuth on Torren IV.

Ratchet is stunned to meet another Lombax.

Azimuth explains that he was one of the Lombaxes who survived when their species disappeared from the universe.

More importantly, he reveals that he knew Ratchet's father, Kaden.

Azimuth recognizes Ratchet as Kaden's son and immediately realizes who he is.

For Ratchet, this is an incredible discovery.

For the first time, he is finally standing face-to-face with someone who knew his father.

Azimuth tells Ratchet more about the disappearance of the Lombaxes.

He explains that when the Lombaxes fled the universe, they left behind technology capable of manipulating time and dimensions.

Ratchet and Azimuth now have the same objective: find Clank and discover how to reach the Great Clock.

Azimuth believes that the Clock could potentially allow them to change the past.

If they could travel back to the moment when the Lombaxes disappeared, they might be able to prevent their extinction and bring Ratchet's people back.

Ratchet sees this as his chance to finally reunite with his family.

The two Lombaxes therefore become partners.

Their first objective is to locate an Obsidian Eye, a Zoni device capable of communicating with the Great Clock.

Their search leads them to the planet Terachnos, where they discover information about the location of an Obsidian Eye hidden on Lumos.

Ratchet and Azimuth eventually reach the device.

Using the Obsidian Eye, they manage to establish contact with Clank inside the Great Clock.

Clank explains that he has learned the truth about Orvus.

He also tells them that Orvus disappeared two years earlier after agreeing to meet Doctor Nefarious.

Clank asks Ratchet to use a time portal to travel back to the past and save his father.

Ratchet agrees.

The heroes travel to Zanifar, where a temporal portal allows Ratchet to travel into the past.

There, he witnesses Doctor Nefarious interrogating Orvus.

Nefarious wants to know how to enter the Orvus Chamber.

He believes that Orvus is the only person capable of giving him access.

But Orvus refuses to reveal the information.

He explains that the Great Clock is not a time machine and must never be used to alter history.

Ratchet attempts to intervene, but he is unable to save Orvus.

Orvus disappears before Nefarious can obtain the information he wants.

Ratchet returns to the present without knowing what ultimately happened to Clank's father.

As Ratchet continues his journey, Azimuth finally reveals a devastating secret.

He was not simply a survivor of the Lombax extinction.

He was partially responsible for it.

Years earlier, Azimuth shared some of the Lombaxes' advanced technology with Emperor Tachyon.

He believed Tachyon could be trusted.

He was wrong.

Tachyon eventually used the technology against the Lombaxes.

Azimuth was blamed for his actions and was exiled.

He has spent years carrying the guilt of what happened.

Now, he believes that the Great Clock is his chance to correct his mistake.

If they can travel back in time, they could stop Tachyon before the Lombaxes were forced to flee.

Ratchet agrees.

For him, the possibility of restoring the Lombax species is worth almost any sacrifice.

But Clank knows something Ratchet does not.

The Great Clock must never be used as a time machine.

While Ratchet and Azimuth continue their search, Doctor Nefarious discovers more about Clank's destiny.

He learns that Clank is destined to become the Senior Caretaker of the Great Clock.

Nefarious realizes that Clank may be the key to accessing the Orvus Chamber.

He therefore launches another attempt to manipulate Clank.

At the same time, Ratchet and Azimuth are attacked by Lord Vorselon, who captures Azimuth and takes him aboard his warship.

Ratchet initially wants to continue searching for Clank.

But he ultimately decides that rescuing Azimuth is more important.

He infiltrates Vorselon's ship and frees his fellow Lombax.

Their relationship grows stronger, and Azimuth begins to act almost like a father figure toward Ratchet.

Meanwhile, Clank continues restoring the Great Clock.

As he learns more about his destiny, he encounters a strange version of the Plumber inside his subconscious.

The Plumber gives him an extremely cryptic warning:

Do not risk more than six minutes.

Clank does not fully understand what this means.

Eventually, Clank and Sigmund manage to unlock the Orvus Chamber.

However, they are immediately betrayed.

Lawrence, Nefarious's loyal butler, has infiltrated the Great Clock.

He attacks Clank and Sigmund and prevents them from reaching the Chamber.

Nefarious then puts another part of his plan into motion.

He sends Clank to Vapedia and creates a fake distress signal.

Ratchet and Azimuth receive the signal and immediately rush to rescue Clank.

But it is a trap.

They arrive on Vapedia and discover that the planet is controlled by Nefarious's Valkyrie forces.

Their leader, Cassiopeia, captures Clank and uses him as bait.

Ratchet and Azimuth fight their way through the Valkyrie fortress.

Eventually, Ratchet defeats Cassiopeia and rescues Clank.

The three finally reunite.

Clank explains the truth about the Great Clock.

He warns Ratchet that the Clock cannot simply be used to rewrite history.

Ratchet begins to understand the danger.

But Azimuth refuses to give up.

He believes that restoring the Lombaxes is worth the risk.

Ratchet eventually decides that he cannot use the Clock to change the past.

This causes a serious disagreement between the two Lombaxes.

Azimuth leaves Ratchet and Clank.

Meanwhile, Doctor Nefarious launches his final plan.

He wants to reach the Great Clock and use it to rewrite history.

His goal is not simply to save himself.

Nefarious wants to create a new timeline in which every villain in history wins and every hero loses.

In this perfect universe for Nefarious, Ratchet, Qwark, and every other hero would never have defeated him.

Nefarious launches a massive assault on the Great Clock.

Ratchet, Clank, and Qwark infiltrate his space station to stop him.

After a massive battle, they manage to destroy Nefarious's armada.

Ratchet and Clank confront Nefarious directly.

The battle ends with Nefarious apparently defeated.

His damaged ship explodes, destroying the space station.

Ratchet and Clank survive and return to the Great Clock.

For the first time, Ratchet believes that everything is finally over.

But Azimuth has not changed his mind.

Ratchet tells Clank that he must stay behind and fulfill his destiny as the Great Clock's Senior Caretaker.

Clank reluctantly accepts.

Ratchet prepares to leave the Clock without his best friend.

But Azimuth suddenly appears.

He has decided that he will use the Great Clock to change history, with or without Ratchet's permission.

Ratchet refuses to help him.

Azimuth becomes enraged.

He attacks Ratchet.

In the struggle, Azimuth shoots Ratchet and kills him.

Clank arrives moments later and finds Ratchet's lifeless body.

Azimuth then runs toward the Orvus Chamber.

His plan is to use the Great Clock to travel back in time and save the Lombaxes.

Clank follows him.

He remembers the Plumber's warning.

Six minutes.

Clank reaches the Orvus Chamber and locks Azimuth out.

He then makes an impossible decision.

He uses the Great Clock to rewind time by exactly six minutes.

The timeline changes.

Ratchet is alive again.

Clank has saved his best friend.

But Azimuth reaches the Orvus Chamber first in the altered timeline.

He activates the Great Clock and begins the temporal shift.

His plan is finally underway.

The Great Clock begins to collapse under the enormous strain.

Ratchet and Clank realize that if the temporal shift continues, the entire universe could be destroyed.

They confront Azimuth.

Ratchet manages to stop him, but the damage to the Clock is already catastrophic.

Azimuth finally realizes what he has done.

He understands that his obsession with correcting his past mistake has put the entire universe at risk.

He apologizes to Ratchet.

Then he makes one final sacrifice.

Azimuth takes control of the damaged systems and stops the temporal shift from destroying the universe.

The effort costs him his life.

Alister Azimuth dies saving the Great Clock and the universe.

The Great Clock is stabilized.

The temporal anomalies throughout the universe disappear.

Ratchet has lost the last Lombax who knew his father, but Azimuth's final act gives him closure.

He finally understands that he cannot bring his people back by destroying the present.

Meanwhile, Clank must now make his own choice.

Orvus had created him to become the Senior Caretaker of the Great Clock.

Clank initially believes that he must stay and fulfill his father's wishes.

Ratchet accepts that he may have to say goodbye to his best friend.

But Clank eventually realizes something important.

His destiny is not simply to maintain the Great Clock.

He has another purpose.

He chooses to remain with Ratchet.

Instead of taking his position as Senior Caretaker, Clank appoints Sigmund as the new Senior Caretaker.

A final message from Orvus confirms that Clank has made the right choice.

Orvus tells his son that he should follow his heart.

Clank therefore leaves the Great Clock and reunites with Ratchet.

The two friends are together once again.

Ratchet has finally learned the truth about his past.

He knows who his father was.

He knows why the Lombaxes disappeared.

He knows that Alister Azimuth sacrificed himself because of his guilt.

And, most importantly, he understands that his future cannot be built by changing the past.

Ratchet and Clank leave the Great Clock together, ready for whatever comes next.

The universe is saved.

The Lombax past remains unchanged.

And the friendship between Ratchet and Clank survives.

However, in a post-credits scene, Captain Qwark is revealed to have survived the destruction of Nefarious's space station.

Unfortunately, he is stranded among the wreckage with his pet war grok, Snowball, who appears to be more interested in eating him than rescuing him.

The adventure ends with Ratchet and Clank reunited once again.

Did you know?

Clank's time-puzzle sections let you record and replay your own past selves.

Modes

  • Story (Solo) — Ratchet looks for Clank, held by Dr. Nefarious at the Great Clock, and meets Alister Azimuth, another Lombax.
  • Challenge Mode (New Game+) — Replay with your arsenal and level weapons further.
  • Agorian Battleplex (Arena) — Arena challenges for bolts and prizes.
  • Space exploration (Exploration) — Fly the Aphelion freely between moons and planets in each sector.
  • Time puzzles (Variations) — Clank records copies of himself to solve time-based puzzles.

Economy

  • Bolts — Main currency, dropped by enemies and crates; spent at Gadgetron vendors on weapons, gadgets and ammo.
  • Gold Bolts — Hidden collectibles that unlock extras.
  • Zoni — Upgrade the Aphelion.
  • Holo-plans — Build special weapons such as the RYNO V.

Progression

  • Weapons level up with use; Constructo weapons can be customised.
  • Skill Points return as optional challenges.
