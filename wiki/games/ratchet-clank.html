🎮 Ratchet & Clank

PlayStation 2 · 2002

PlatformPlayStation 2
Year2002
PlayableRatchet, Clank
Release4 November 2002 (NA) · 8 November 2002 (EU)
DeveloperInsomniac Games

On planet Veldin, a Lombax mechanic named Ratchet dreams of space travel — until a small defective robot he nicknames Clank crash-lands nearby. Clank reveals that Chairman Drek of the Blarg is harvesting chunks of inhabited planets to build a new world. The unlikely duo set off across the Solana Galaxy to stop him.

Story

On the planet Veldin, a young Lombax mechanic named Ratchet dreams of exploring other star systems and builds a spacecraft with the help of the Gadgetron assistant.

On an unknown planet, inside an assembly plant producing gigantic war robots, a malfunction occurs and results in the creation of a small robot, numbered B5429761, who appears to possess a consciousness of his own. Shortly after leaving the production line, he finds an InfoBot and is horrified by the recording it contains. The other robots then spot him and attempt to eliminate him, but he manages to escape aboard a spacecraft. However, the shuttle is shot down and crashes on Veldin.

Ratchet, who witnessed the crash, explores the wreckage and gets rid of the robots sent to eliminate its pilot. He eventually finds the small robot and brings him home. There, the robot shows him the InfoBot he discovered. It contains a message from Supreme Executive Chairman Drek, leader of the Blarg, addressed to the planet Novalis.

Drek explains that the Blarg homeworld, Orxon, is suffering from pollution and overpopulation. To solve the problem, Drek reveals his plan: to build a new planet for his species by extracting entire sections from other planets, including Novalis. Such a process could result in the destruction of the planets involved, something Drek cynically admits he is fully aware of.

At the end of the message, the little robot asks Ratchet to help him find Captain Qwark, a famous superhero whom Ratchet admires and who might be able to stop Drek. Ratchet explains that he cannot start his spacecraft because it is missing an essential component: a robotic ignition system. The little robot reveals that he is equipped with such a system.

The duo therefore sets off for Novalis, and during the journey Ratchet gives the robot the name Clank.

Ratchet is thrilled to finally leave his planet, but his excitement is short-lived: their spacecraft crashes on Novalis. There, they repel the Blarg invasion and rescue the President, who lends them a new spacecraft and tells them that Captain Qwark is in Metropolis, on the planet Kerwan, filming a commercial.

The two companions set out to find him, leading them to explore several planets, including Kerwan, where they meet the engineer Al; the forest planet Eudora, where they confront one of Drek's lieutenants; the swamps of Aridia, where they rescue hoverboard champion Skid McMarx; as well as a Blarg research space station.

Ratchet and Clank eventually pick up Qwark's trail in Blackwater City, on the planet Rilgar, where an hoverboard race is being held. The winner will have the privilege of meeting the superhero.

The two companions make their way to Qwark's private suite and tell him about Drek's plans. To their surprise, Qwark reveals that he already knew about them. He is also impressed by their efforts to reach him and offers to team up with them.

He gives them the coordinates of his headquarters on Umbris, a fortress with supposedly impenetrable defenses that Ratchet and Clank must cross to prove their worth. After overcoming the trials, they finally meet Qwark.

Ratchet becomes increasingly suspicious of Qwark, while Clank remains completely confident in the superhero. Qwark, however, betrays them by tricking them into falling into the pit of a Blargian monster.

Qwark then reveals that, in order to finance his comeback as a superhero, he became Drek's spokesperson and therefore has no intention of stopping him. He leaves the two companions as prey for the monster, but Ratchet defeats it and escapes from the pit.

Ratchet is furious about being betrayed and wants to pursue Qwark for revenge. However, the duo comes across an InfoBot showing a commando unit under siege by Drek's forces on the planet Batalia.

Obsessed with getting revenge on Qwark, Ratchet refuses to help them. Clank, who understands that Drek is a far greater threat than Qwark, insists that they go and provide assistance.

Unable to start the spacecraft without the little robot's help, Ratchet is forced to agree. However, he remains largely unconcerned about the threat Drek poses to the galaxy and becomes cold and hostile toward Clank, even threatening to send him to the scrap heap.

His attitude gradually begins to change as they continue their journey, particularly when he learns that Drek plans to destroy an entire planet called Hoven, simply because it occupies the perfect orbit for the new Blarg planet.

Ratchet then announces that although his main goal is still to find Qwark, he is willing to stop Drek along the way. The duo therefore takes the time to destroy the Deplanetizer, the weapon Drek intended to use to destroy the planet.

After Ratchet and Clank prevent the destruction of Hoven, Drek decides to send Qwark after them once again.

Ratchet and Clank encounter Qwark at the Gemlik Base, near the planet Oltanis, and face him once again in a space battle. Ratchet shoots down Qwark's spacecraft, which crashes on Oltanis.

However, when they return to Gemlik, they find an InfoBot and discover that Drek's army has devastated the planet Oltanis.

Ratchet realizes that he has been selfish and that he should have stopped Drek, the true threat, instead of pursuing Qwark simply for revenge.

He regrets allowing Drek to devastate entire planets without consequence and realizes that he and Clank are the galaxy's last hope.

He therefore decides to join forces with Clank and stop the Blarg tyrant as quickly as possible.

Following Drek's trail, the duo arrives on the planet Quartu, which turns out to be the planet where Clank was created at the beginning of the game.

The two companions follow the robot assembly line and eventually find Clank's mother: the computer that created him.

She creates an InfoBot for them, revealing the coordinates of Drek's fleet and his new target: Veldin.

Drek has chosen to destroy Ratchet's home planet because it occupies the ideal orbit for the new Blarg planet.

Furious, Ratchet infiltrates Drek's fleet but realizes that he is too late: Drek is already on Veldin, preparing to activate his new weapon of mass destruction, the Deplanetizer.

Ratchet returns to Veldin and confronts Drek, who is piloting a gigantic battle robot.

Their duel takes them to the Deplanetizer, where Drek reveals that he was responsible for the pollution of his own planet, which he caused solely to make money.

After contaminating Orxon, he created an artificial planet and sold it to the Blarg at an enormous price. Now that every piece of his new world has been sold, he intends to pollute it as well and repeat the same scheme all over again.

Ratchet manages to defeat Drek. The tyrant is catapulted onto his artificial planet, which Ratchet destroys using the Deplanetizer.

The explosion sends debris flying everywhere, and both heroes fall from the Deplanetizer's platform.

Clank manages to catch Ratchet while holding onto the edge of the platform, but the effort required to hold onto his friend severely damages his arm, and the two heroes fall into the void.

Clank saves Ratchet from a fatal fall using his Helipack.

Ratchet then heads back home, while Clank, believing that the Lombax no longer wants him around, walks away on his own.

However, Ratchet catches up with him, supposedly to repair his damaged arm.

Clank then realizes that this is the beginning of a great friendship between them, and the two heroes return to Ratchet's garage, awaiting a new adventure.

Did you know?

Ratchet was first imagined as a space-travelling reptile — feline features won out for their sense of agility.

Modes

  • Story (Solo) — Ratchet and Clank cross the Solana galaxy to stop Chairman Drek from building a new planet out of pieces of other worlds.
  • Challenge Mode (New Game+) — Unlocked after the credits: replay the whole game keeping your weapons and bolts, against tougher enemies.
  • Hoverboard races (Mini-game) — Hoverboard races against local racers, with prizes for the winner.
    Maps: Rilgar, Kalebo III
  • Giant Clank & space battles (Variations) — Some planets swap the platforming for ship dogfights or Clank-only sections.

Economy

  • Bolts — Main currency, dropped by enemies and crates; spent at Gadgetron vendors on weapons, gadgets and ammo.
  • Gold Bolts — 40 hidden collectibles, used to unlock the gold version of weapons.
  • Infobots — Found as the story advances; each one unlocks the coordinates of a new planet.

Progression

  • Weapons and gadgets are bought from Gadgetron vendors; they do not level up yet in this first game.
  • Skill Points: 30 hidden challenges that unlock extras.
  • The RYNO, the most expensive weapon of the game, is sold for 150,000 bolts.
