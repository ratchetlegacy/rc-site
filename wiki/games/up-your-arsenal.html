🎮 Up Your Arsenal

PlayStation 2 · 2004

PlatformPlayStation 2
Year2004
PlayableRatchet, Clank, Captain Qwark
Release2 November 2004 (NA) · 12 November 2004 (EU)
DeveloperInsomniac Games

Back in Solana, the galaxy faces the robotic menace of Dr. Nefarious. Ratchet joins the Q-Force alongside a washed-up Captain Qwark, blending frantic combat with sharp comedy.

Story

Since saving the Bogon Galaxy, Ratchet and Clank are once again out of work. Clank has become the star of a television series called Secret Agent Clank, making him more popular than Ratchet.

The two friends are relaxing in their apartment in Megapolis, on the planet Endako, when they learn that Veldin is under attack by the Tyranoids, an alien species led by Doctor Nefarious, a robotic scientist who wants to eliminate all organic life and establish a robotic empire throughout the galaxy.

Ratchet returns to the Solana Galaxy to help the Galactic Rangers, who immediately promote him to the rank of sergeant.

He is then contacted by President Phyronix, who is delighted to have "Agent Clank" on their side and asks the two companions to find the only person who has defeated Nefarious in the past.

The heroes travel to the lush planet Florana, where they discover that the person they are looking for is none other than Captain Qwark.

However, Qwark has lost his memory and believes himself to be a monkey from Florana.

Ratchet and Clank are then contacted by Sasha, President Phyronix's daughter and captain of the Phoenix, a gigantic spacecraft that will serve as the duo's base of operations.

Ratchet and Clank officially join the fight against Nefarious, and the President explains that Qwark's help is essential, meaning they must help him regain his memory.

Ratchet and Clank succeed by playing Vid-Comics, video games loosely based on Qwark's so-called "adventures."

As Qwark watches his exploits on screen, his memory returns. He then forms an elite team to fight Nefarious called the Q-Force.

The group is led by Qwark and consists of Ratchet, Clank, Sasha, hoverboard champion Skid McMarx, robotic coach Helga, who trains Ratchet in the use of gadgets, inventor Al, and Skrunch, a Florana monkey who has become Qwark's best friend.

Qwark first sends Ratchet, Clank, and Skid to infiltrate Nefarious's base on Aquatos.

He then sends our two heroes to attack the Tyranoid homeworld, Tyranosis, while the rest of the team "supervises the situation" from the Phoenix — meaning they enjoy Fry Day on Deck 5.

After defeating the Tyranoid Queen, our two heroes inspect Nefarious's base on the planet Daxx, where they discover that Nefarious is working with the famous robotic singer Courtney Gears.

Clank attempts to approach her by inviting her to the filming of an episode of Secret Agent Clank in order to obtain information from her.

However, the singer captures him and delivers him to Nefarious.

Nefarious attempts to recruit Clank to his cause and encourages him to turn against organic beings.

Clank replies that both robots and biological life have the right to exist and refuses the offer.

Nefarious therefore keeps him captive and sends a robot identical to Clank, named Klunk, to take his place beside Ratchet, who is completely fooled.

Klunk, programmed by Nefarious, tells the Q-Force that the robotic scientist is aboard a galactic cruiser known as the Leviathan, claiming that he obtained this information from Courtney Gears.

At this point, Skid, who has been investigating the moons of Obani, is captured by the singer.

Ratchet attempts to rescue him and discovers Nefarious's plan: to transform all organic life into robots using a machine known as the Biobliterator.

Ratchet is unable to prevent Skid from being transformed into a robot, but he manages to eliminate Courtney Gears.

Following this, the Q-Force decides to infiltrate the Leviathan, which they have located in the atmosphere of Zeldren, in the hope of capturing Nefarious.

Ratchet, Klunk, Qwark, and Skrunch travel to the cruiser aboard a shuttle and indeed find Nefarious, unaware that they have fallen into his trap.

Nefarious activates the self-destruct sequence of his own ship before teleporting away.

Ratchet, Klunk, and Skrunch return to their shuttle, but Qwark insists on searching Nefarious's office because he believes he saw something important there.

However, Qwark takes too long to return, and Klunk takes off with the shuttle, claiming that they can no longer wait.

The heroes escape the Leviathan without Qwark, and the cruiser explodes.

Back aboard the Phoenix, the survivors hold a funeral for Captain Qwark.

Ratchet plays the Vid-Comics to learn more about Nefarious's plans.

He discovers that the mad scientist had intended to destroy Metropolis, on the planet Kerwan, but that Qwark had stopped him.

Ratchet realizes that Metropolis is Nefarious's next target and immediately heads there.

Once again, he arrives too late and is unable to prevent Nefarious from turning the city into robots.

However, he finds the real Clank and destroys Klunk.

With the entire galaxy in despair and expecting to be transformed into robots, Ratchet and Clank decide to investigate the crash site of the Leviathan on Zeldren to find clues about what Qwark was searching for before his supposed death.

They discover an escape pod and an InfoBot, realizing that Qwark actually managed to escape the Leviathan.

They also find a data disk containing what Qwark discovered aboard the cruiser.

The two companions eventually locate Qwark in his secret hideout in the Thran Asteroid Belt.

Qwark explains that coming so close to death frightened him and that he no longer wants to oppose Nefarious, claiming that it is not his place to decide who should or should not be turned into a robot.

Ratchet reminds him that he once admired him, while Clank tells him that the galaxy believes in him and that he can restore its hope and finally become the hero he has always wanted to be.

Meanwhile, Al deciphers the data disk recovered by Qwark and discovers that the Biobliterator is located on the planet Koros.

Ratchet and Clank destroy the machine, but the disk reveals the existence of a second, more powerful weapon on the planet Mylon.

Ratchet and Clank confront Nefarious, who eventually takes refuge inside his machine, which transforms into a gigantic battle robot.

Ratchet and Clank are joined by Qwark for the final battle.

The battle ends with the machine exploding, ejecting Nefarious and his butler Lawrence onto an asteroid.

Afterwards, the heroes, along with all the supporting characters from the three games, attend the premiere of the new holo-film, Secret Agent Clank.

Did you know?

This is the debut of Dr. Nefarious and his long-suffering butler Lawrence.

Modes

  • Story (Solo) — Back in Solana, Ratchet joins the Q-Force to stop Dr. Nefarious and his plan to turn all organics into robots.
  • Challenge Mode (New Game+) — Replay with all your weapons; weapons can be upgraded further.
  • Qwark Vid-Comics (Mini-game) — 2D side-scrolling levels starring Captain Qwark, unlocked as the story advances.
  • Annihilation Nation (Arena) — Televised arena challenges with bolt and item rewards.
  • Online multiplayer (Multiplayer) — Siege, Capture the Flag and Deathmatch, the first online multiplayer in the series.

Economy

  • Bolts — Main currency, dropped by enemies and crates; spent at Gadgetron vendors on weapons, gadgets and ammo.
  • Titanium Bolts — Hidden collectibles that unlock extras.
  • Weapon mods — Bought from vendors to add effects to weapons.

Progression

  • Weapons level up with use up to V5.
  • Ratchet's health grows with experience.
  • Skill Points return as optional challenges.
