🎮 Going Commando

PlayStation 2 · 2003

PlatformPlayStation 2
Year2003
PlayableRatchet, Clank
Release11 November 2003 (NA) · 21 November 2003 (EU)
DeveloperInsomniac Games

The heroes are whisked to the Bogon Galaxy, hired by the mysterious Megacorp to recover a stolen experiment. Deeper, funnier and more action-packed, with a darker secret lurking behind the corporation.

Story

Several months have passed since Ratchet and Clank defeated President Drek. While Clank seems to be adjusting to a more peaceful life, Ratchet misses the days when he was fighting the Blarg tyrant and complains that his life has become far too quiet.

While they are on Veldin, being interviewed for the television show Behind the Heroes, the two friends are suddenly teleported aboard a spacecraft located in the distant Bogon Galaxy.

There, they are contacted by Abercrombie Fizzwidget, the head of MegaCorp, a company that produces all kinds of gadgets, much like Gadgetron.

Fizzwidget explains that he noticed the two heroes following their exploits in the Solana Galaxy and wants to entrust them with an urgent mission: a top-secret experimental pet created by MegaCorp, known as the Protopet, has been kidnapped by a masked thief.

Fizzwidget wants Ratchet to recover the creature. Clank, meanwhile, is offered a job at MegaCorp and a luxurious apartment in Megapolis, on Endako.

The two companions part ways, and after two weeks of intensive training, Ratchet is ready for his mission.

He infiltrates the thief's base, located by Fizzwidget: a flying laboratory on the planet Aranos. However, Ratchet is discovered by the thief before he can recover the Protopet and is forced to flee.

Following leads provided by Fizzwidget, Ratchet tracks the thief to the planet Oozla, and then to Maktar Nebula, where he discovers that the thief has hired a group of mercenaries known as Thugs-4-Less for protection.

Ratchet also receives a message from the thief revealing that Clank has been captured as a warning.

Ratchet frees Clank in Megapolis, and the two friends are reunited.

As they travel across the galaxy, they pick up episodes of Behind the Heroes dedicated to Captain Qwark, revealing more about what happened to him after the events of the first game.

It is revealed that Qwark sold counterfeit Gadgetron products, including the Personal Hygienator, which turned out to be defective and caused its users to suffer from severe itching.

Qwark was subsequently ordered to pay six billion bolts in damages.

He then attempted to escape but was arrested on Pokitaru and imprisoned. Apparently, he escaped through the prison toilets before disappearing into the wilderness.

Ratchet and Clank travel to the planet Barlow to infiltrate the Thugs-4-Less biker gang, the Desert Riders.

They become friendly with one of the bikers, who shows them a message from the leader of the Thugs ordering his troops to gather in the Feltzin System.

Ratchet and Clank travel there and defeat the Thugs during a space battle. Afterwards, they intercept a communication between the thief and the leader of the Thugs.

The thief reprimands the mercenary and orders the Thugs to meet in Canal City, on Notak.

The two companions arrive too late, but discover the location of the thief's base on Siberius.

They confront the thief, who is about to destroy the Protopet, and manage to save the creature. The thief, however, escapes.

Fizzwidget then arranges to meet the two heroes on the planet Tabora, in a desert mining region, to retrieve the Protopet.

However, on the journey back, Ratchet and Clank are ejected from the director's spacecraft.

Ratchet assumes that the absent-minded businessman simply made a mistake, but Clank is more suspicious.

While the two companions are wandering through a tunnel on Tabora, the thief catches up with them, having followed them to recover the Protopet.

However, she accidentally falls from her spacecraft, causing her mask to come off.

The thief is revealed to be a Lombax named Angela Cross.

When she learns that Fizzwidget has the Protopet, she gives the two heroes the coordinates of a MegaCorp product testing facility on Dobbo.

There, they discover a report written by scientists who tested the Protopet.

The report reveals that, despite its harmless and docile appearance, the Protopet is extremely aggressive. The scientists recommend exterminating the specimen.

Ratchet and Clank realize that they are the only ones who have seen the report and conclude that Fizzwidget himself is unaware of the truth.

They attempt to contact him to warn him.

From that point onward, Fizzwidget becomes the one the two companions pursue across the galaxy.

They attempt to reach him in the Hrugis Cloud, where they nearly get shot down because of an incorrect code provided by Fizzwidget.

They then travel to the planet Todano, where Fizzwidget has filmed a commercial for the MegaCorp weapons factory.

There, Angela tells them that the Thugs have a new employer who offered them a better deal, and that her laboratory on Aranos has been taken over.

Ratchet and Clank travel to Silver City, on Boldan, in an attempt to finally reach Fizzwidget, but it turns out to be a trap.

The two friends discover that the Thugs have been hired by MegaCorp to protect Fizzwidget.

Ratchet and Clank are captured by the Thugs and taken to Angela's laboratory, which has been converted into a prison.

They are rescued by Clank's robotic girlfriend, but learn that Angela has herself been captured by the Thugs.

The two heroes set out to rescue her, destroying the Thugs' fleet along the way.

The leader of the Thugs escapes with Angela to Snivelak, where Ratchet and Clank catch up with him and defeat him after a titanic battle.

Angela then reveals that she used to work in MegaCorp's genetic division and was on the verge of correcting the Protopet's genetic defects when Fizzwidget decided to move up its release date.

The three companions infiltrate a MegaCorp distribution center on Smolg, where they discover that MegaCorp has created tens of thousands of Protopets, foreshadowing a catastrophic disaster.

Our heroes even witness the terrible destruction caused by the Protopets in Allgon City, on Damosel.

Angela finally explains that the only way to stop the threat is to correct the original Protopet—the only specimen capable of being duplicated—in order to halt the invasion.

Ratchet, Clank, and Angela arrive at the MegaCorp factory on Yeedil.

Clank's robotic girlfriend then arrives and informs them that Captain Qwark, whom nobody had heard from for some time, has spent six weeks selling Personal Hygienators under the alias Robert MacQwark, in order to finance his superhero comeback.

That is when Fizzwidget appears and captures the heroes, only to reveal that he is actually Qwark in disguise.

Qwark had captured the real Fizzwidget, hired Ratchet and Clank, organized the distribution of defective Protopets, recruited Thugs-4-Less, and lured Ratchet, Clank, and Angela to Yeedil solely to trap them.

After being discredited throughout the Solana Galaxy following the events of the first game, Qwark decided to restart his superhero career in a new galaxy and set his sights on the Bogon Galaxy.

His plan is to cure the Protopet live on television, as Captain Qwark rather than Abercrombie Fizzwidget, allowing him to become the new hero of the galaxy by eliminating a threat that he himself created.

At the same time, he intends to frame Ratchet and Clank, whom he blames for his downfall in the Solana Galaxy, as well as Angela, the only other person aware of his plan, for the Protopet crisis.

This would allow him to get rid of all three of them with legitimate justification.

Qwark takes the Helixomorph, a device created by Angela capable of removing the Protopet's murderous instincts, and uses it on the creature.

However, Angela's device does not work as expected. The Protopet grows to a monstrous size and devours Qwark.

Ratchet and Clank fight the Protopet and eventually defeat it.

The defeated creature then spits Qwark out, and Qwark himself spits out the Helixomorph.

Angela repairs the device—the battery had simply been inserted upside down—and uses it to cure the Protopet.

Ratchet, Clank, Angela, and Clank's robotic companion then head back to Megapolis to rest, while Qwark becomes a MegaCorp product tester.

Did you know?

This entry introduced weapon leveling — the more you used a gun, the stronger it grew.

Modes

  • Story (Solo) — Abercrombie Fizzwidget hires Ratchet and Clank to recover a stolen experiment in the Bogon galaxy.
  • Challenge Mode (New Game+) — Replay with all your gear; a bolt multiplier rises as you defeat enemies without taking damage, and weapons can reach their Mega version.
  • Arena challenges (Mini-game) — Gladiator-style arena fights with bolt and item prizes.
  • Hoverbike races (Mini-game) — High-speed hoverbike courses with prizes for the best times.
  • Space combat (Mini-game) — Missions aboard Ratchet's ship against fighters and capital ships.

Economy

  • Bolts — Main currency, dropped by enemies and crates; spent at Gadgetron vendors on weapons, gadgets and ammo.
  • Raritanium — Rare mineral used to upgrade Ratchet's ship.
  • Platinum Bolts — Hidden collectibles traded for weapon modifications.
  • Nanotech — Health upgrades: Ratchet's maximum health grows with experience and can be boosted further.

Progression

  • Weapons level up with use and evolve into a stronger version (the first game in the series to do this).
  • Ratchet gains experience and maximum Nanotech as he fights.
  • Skill Points return as optional challenges.
