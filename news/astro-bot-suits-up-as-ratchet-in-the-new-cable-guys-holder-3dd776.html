Astro Bot suits up as Ratchet in the new Cable Guys holder

October 2026 · JB Hi-Fi

Cable Guys has added an unexpected crossover to its line-up: a Ratchet Bot stand, where Astro Bot appears in an oversized Ratchet helmet and outfit. It’s officially licensed by Sony, and it’s listed under the Ratchet & Clank franchise — a first for a series that rarely gets new merchandise these days.

Beyond the figure itself, it’s a working desk accessory. The stand holds a phone, a remote or a controller, and it’s compatible with the PS5 DualSense, the Xbox Series S and X pads, the Switch and Switch 2, as well as most smartphones. A one-year manufacturer’s warranty is included; the phone shown in the product images isn’t.

It’s currently listed at JB Hi-Fi in Australia for A$44 — roughly €25 — under SKU 911769. No word yet on a European release, so collectors outside Australia will have to watch import listings for now.

Worth noting for completists: this is an Astro Bot figure wearing a Ratchet costume, not a Ratchet figure. Whether it belongs in a Ratchet & Clank collection is a question each collector will answer differently — but Sony’s own classification puts it there.
