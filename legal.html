When you browse the site

  • Audience measurement: for each page, the site records the page viewed, the site you came from, your time zone, your language and your type of device (mobile, tablet or computer). No IP address, cookie or visitor identifier is recorded.
  • Visitor counter: your visitor number is kept in your own browser (local storage) so the site can greet you again; the site only stores the total number of visits.
  • Preferences: your browser also keeps your language, whether animation sounds are on, and similar settings. They never leave your device.
