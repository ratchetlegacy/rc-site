🎮 The Games
Two decades of intergalactic adventure, from the PS2 original to the PS5's dimension-hopping showcase.
2002

Ratchet & Clank

PlayStation 2

The one that started it all. A Lombax mechanic and a tiny defective robot team up to stop the villainous Chairman Drek from destroying planets.

2003

Going Commando

PlayStation 2 · aka Locked and Loaded

A brand-new galaxy, bigger arsenal and deeper upgrades. The duo is hired by Megacorp — and uncovers a darker secret behind the company.

2004

Up Your Arsenal

PlayStation 2 · aka R&C 3

The debut of Dr. Nefarious. A fan-favourite blend of combat, comedy and charm that shaped the series for years.

2005

Deadlocked

PlayStation 2 · aka Gladiator

Ratchet is forced into deadly gladiator battles. The most combat-focused entry, leaning into shooter territory.

2007

Size Matters

PSP (later PS2)

A handheld adventure developed by High Impact Games, packing a full R&C experience into your pocket.

2007

Tools of Destruction

PlayStation 3 · Future saga

The Future saga begins. Ratchet hunts for the truth about the Lombax race while fleeing Emperor Tachyon.

2008

Quest for Booty

PlayStation 3

A shorter, story-driven chapter bridging Tools of Destruction and A Crack in Time.

2009

A Crack in Time

PlayStation 3

The emotional culmination of the Future trilogy, with time manipulation, space exploration and one of the series' best stories.

2011

All 4 One

PlayStation 3 · Co-op

Four-player co-op chaos with Ratchet, Clank, Qwark and Nefarious all playable together.

2012

Full Frontal Assault

PlayStation 3 · aka Q-Force

A tower-defense twist on the formula, mixing action with base defence.

2013

Into the Nexus

PlayStation 3 · aka Nexus

A return to classic form and the close of the Future saga.

2016

Ratchet & Clank (2016)

PlayStation 4 · Reimagining

A gorgeous reimagining of the original, released alongside the animated movie.

2021

Rift Apart

PlayStation 5

A dimension-hopping showcase for the PS5, introducing Rivet and pushing the series to stunning new heights.

📖 Lore & History
Where the series came from, and the story threads that tie the galaxy together.

🏭 Made by Insomniac

Insomniac Games created Ratchet & Clank in 2002 and has developed most mainline entries. The series became one of PlayStation's most beloved and enduring platformer-shooters.

🐾 The Lombax mystery

The Future saga digs into Ratchet's origins and the fate of the Lombax race — a species of intelligent, technologically gifted felinoids who vanished from the galaxy.

🎮 The trilogy & spin-offs

The PS2 trilogy defined the series. High Impact Games later crafted handheld spin-offs on PSP, expanding the universe beyond the main console line.

🎬 Movie & reimagining

In 2016, Insomniac released a reimagining of the first game on PS4 alongside an animated feature film — a fresh take on the origin story.

🌌 Rift Apart's dimensions

Rift Apart (2021) introduced dimensional rifts and Rivet, using the PS5's power for near-instant travel between shattered realities.

🔫 Weapons & gadgets

From the Groovitron to the RYNO, the series is famous for its inventive, over-the-top arsenal — a core part of R&C's identity and humour.
