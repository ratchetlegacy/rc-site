🎮 The Games
Two decades of intergalactic adventure, from the PS2 original to the PS5's dimension-hopping showcase.
2002

Ratchet & Clank

PlayStation 2
▾

The one that started it all. On the planet Veldin, a Lombax mechanic named Ratchet dreams of space travel — until a small defective robot he nicknames Clank crash-lands nearby. Clank reveals that Chairman Drek of the Blarg is harvesting chunks of inhabited planets to build a new world for his polluted race. The unlikely duo set off across the Solana Galaxy, building a wild arsenal to stop him.

💡Did you know? Insomniac had just finished the Spyro trilogy. Ratchet was first imagined as a space-travelling reptile — feline features won out for their sense of agility.
2003

Going Commando

PS2 · aka Locked and Loaded
▾

The heroes are whisked to the brand-new Bogon Galaxy, hired by the mysterious Megacorp to recover a stolen experiment. A bigger arsenal, weapon upgrades and RPG-style stats turned the sequel into a turning point — deeper, funnier and more action-packed, with a darker secret lurking behind the corporation.

💡Did you know? This entry introduced weapon leveling — the more you used a gun, the stronger it grew. It became a series staple.
2004

Up Your Arsenal

PS2 · aka R&C 3
▾

Back in Solana, the galaxy faces the robotic menace of Dr. Nefarious. Ratchet joins the Q-Force alongside a washed-up Captain Qwark, blending frantic combat with some of the series' sharpest comedy. Widely regarded as one of the very best entries, it shaped the tone of everything that followed.

💡Did you know? This is the debut of Dr. Nefarious and his long-suffering butler Lawrence — instantly the franchise's most iconic villains.
2005

Deadlocked

PS2 · aka Gladiator
▾

Ratchet is kidnapped and forced to compete in DreadZone, a deadly televised gladiator tournament. The most combat-focused classic entry, it leaned hard into shooter territory with two combat bots at your side, arena challenges and a heavy-metal attitude.

💡Did you know? Clank sits most of this one out — instead of adventuring together, he's Ratchet's mission-control voice from the sidelines.
2007

Size Matters

PSP · later PS2
▾

Developed by High Impact Games, this handheld adventure packed a full-sized R&C experience onto the PSP. Ratchet and Clank investigate the mystery of the Technomites, a secretive race of tiny inventors, across a surprisingly meaty campaign.

💡Did you know? It was later ported to PS2 — proof the handheld entries were more than just spin-offs.
2007

Tools of Destruction

PlayStation 3 · Future saga
▾

The Future saga begins in glorious HD. Pursued by the ruthless Emperor Tachyon, Ratchet flees Solana and starts to uncover the truth about the vanished Lombax race. Gorgeous visuals, inventive gadgets and a heartfelt mystery marked the series' leap to a new generation.

💡Did you know? This is where the deep Lombax lore really begins — the emotional backbone of the entire Future trilogy.
2008

Quest for Booty

PlayStation 3
▾

A shorter, downloadable chapter bridging Tools of Destruction and A Crack in Time. Ratchet searches for a way to rescue Clank, in a more story-driven, exploration-focused adventure with a pirate-tinged flavour.

💡Did you know? At about a third the length of a full game, it was one of the series' first experiments with bite-sized digital releases.
2009

A Crack in Time

PlayStation 3
▾

The emotional culmination of the Future trilogy. Ratchet searches for Clank while Clank discovers his own destiny at the Great Clock, learning to manipulate time itself. Space exploration, clever puzzles and a genuinely moving story make it a fan favourite — and for many, the series' peak.

💡Did you know? Clank's time-puzzle sections let you record and replay your own past selves to solve rooms — a bold, brainy twist.
2011

All 4 One

PlayStation 3 · Co-op
▾

A four-player co-op spin-off where Ratchet, Clank, Qwark and even Dr. Nefarious must team up. It swapped the usual formula for fixed camera angles and party-style teamwork — a lighter, more chaotic take built for playing with friends.

💡Did you know? Watching arch-enemies Qwark and Nefarious forced to cooperate is half the fun — the bickering never stops.
2012

Full Frontal Assault

PS3 · aka Q-Force
▾

A compact entry blending classic action with tower-defense strategy. Players defend their base while pushing out to capture enemy territory — an experimental detour that split the difference between genres.

💡Did you know? It was one of the shortest and most budget-friendly releases, aimed at digital players.
2013

Into the Nexus

PS3 · aka Nexus
▾

A return to classic, story-driven form and the close of the Future saga. Ratchet faces the dangerous Vendra Prog while the game leans into a slightly darker, more heartfelt tone — a fond farewell to the PS3 era.

💡Did you know? Despite its shorter length, fans praised it as a return to what made the classics great.
2016

Ratchet & Clank (2016)

PlayStation 4 · Reimagining
▾

A stunning ground-up reimagining of the original, released alongside the animated movie. It retells the first adventure — narrated by Qwark — with modern visuals, new weapons and reworked planets. A perfect entry point for newcomers.

💡Did you know? Because Qwark narrates, the story playfully contradicts itself — fans treat the 2002 original as the "true" canon version.
2021

Rift Apart

PlayStation 5
▾

A dimension-hopping showcase for the PS5. Dr. Nefarious's meddling tears open rifts between realities, introducing Rivet — a battle-hardened Lombax from another dimension. Near-instant loading, dazzling visuals and heartfelt storytelling pushed the series to spectacular new heights.

💡Did you know? The rifts use the PS5's SSD to yank you between whole worlds in a blink — a feature that was impossible on earlier hardware.

📖 Lore & History
Where the series came from, and the story threads that tie the galaxy together.

🏭 Made by Insomniac

Insomniac Games created Ratchet & Clank in 2002 and has developed most mainline entries. The series became one of PlayStation's most beloved and enduring platformer-shooters.

🐾 The Lombax mystery

The Future saga digs into Ratchet's origins and the fate of the Lombax race — a species of intelligent, technologically gifted felinoids who vanished from the galaxy.

🎮 The trilogy & spin-offs

The PS2 trilogy defined the series. High Impact Games later crafted handheld spin-offs on PSP, expanding the universe beyond the main console line.

🎬 Movie & reimagining

In 2016, Insomniac released a reimagining of the first game on PS4 alongside an animated feature film — a fresh take on the origin story.

🌌 Rift Apart's dimensions

Rift Apart (2021) introduced dimensional rifts and Rivet, using the PS5's power for near-instant travel between shattered realities.

🔫 Weapons & gadgets

From the Groovitron to the RYNO, the series is famous for its inventive, over-the-top arsenal — a core part of R&C's identity and humour.
